**Status:** PARKED — research and planning only. No changes to canonical Day Zero content or production artifacts.

## Commercial hypothesis

Day Zero is the free, high-value top-of-funnel entry point for the BOSS educational framework. Days 1–7 may become paid instruction. Optional continuing membership, academy implementation services, and platform partnerships are separate downstream experiments; no pricing or revenue forecast is approved as canonical.

| Model | What it permits conceptually | Unverified gates |

| Affiliate/referral | Recommend an LMS to qualified graduates | Attribution, commissions, eligibility, payout |

**Entity status:** No LLC is established for this project; do not assume a company exists or mix in unrelated businesses. Clarify vendor eligibility for individuals/sole proprietors before recommending formation.
**Costs and paperwork:** Not confirmed for reseller path. Request written partner terms, fee schedule, ID/tax documentation requirements, payout arrangements, learner data processing terms, refund/support obligations.
