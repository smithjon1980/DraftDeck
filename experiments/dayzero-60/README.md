## Revised output contract

**Production master: layered PDF (16:9), not a PowerPoint-led shape composition.** Preserve native selectable/editable PDF text for story and editorial headings, keep the approved original Bioscillate logo on the bottom right, and retain the exact cyan `#00BCEB`, magenta `#E30074`, black and white brand system. Original engineered illustrations may be embedded as separate image assets with their own provenance and license. CAD reference annotation and text should remain separate native PDF objects. An embedded picture is not itself an editable CAD model; PDF text editability does not imply that detailed art is vector-editable.

Each story slide requires **three independently reviewable layers**:
1. **Story:** real source-grounded Main Street Copy scene, event, conflict or unknown, observed consequence, and reflective question. Use exact source excerpts where appropriate; distinguish paraphrase from quotation. Do not invent customer correspondence or claim classroom execution.
2. **Art:** detailed relevant visual narrative (shop scene, machine process, inspected sheets, source ledger, error/hold mechanism), using carefully constructed CAD/ISO drafting perspectives, expressive black-ink illustration, engineered line weights, construction lines, hatching, part references, limited cyan/magenta signals, and a technical-figure caption. Generic boxes, timelines, glossy dashboards, or decorative wavy abstractions alone do **not** qualify.
3. **Information:** premium Tesla-center-display-inspired typographic hierarchy, visually restrained panes, source provenance, figure/callout connections, annotation and engineering references, notes/reflection; all selectable/editable PDF text.

**NC styling:** technical operation/sequence/register detailing is a stylistic cue, not a claim that the illustration is a functional numerical-control manufacturing drawing. Use **NTS** for diagrams lacking verified measurements. Do not fabricate real part dimensions, code, tolerances or physical machine specifications.
