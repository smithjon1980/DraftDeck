## Sequence
1. `skills/market-demand-screen` — choose testable segment; unsourced demand => HOLD.
2. `skills/value-equation-assessment` — identify desired outcomes, perceived probability, wait time and effort; this is not financial ROI.
3. `skills/problem-solution-map` — map actual obstacles and alternatives including manual optimization.
4. `skills/offer-scope-package` — assemble economically feasible bounded delivery scope.
5. `skills/ethical-risk-reversal` — qualify guarantees and obligations; unfulfillable promises => HOLD.
6. `skills/honest-offer-presentation` — write supported, comprehensible offer and inspect public claims.
