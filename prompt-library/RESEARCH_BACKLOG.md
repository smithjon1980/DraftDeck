## Admission policy

**Do not** promote a remembered title or a retrospective summary into a verified source transcript. For an archival claim of VERBATIM, fetch the actual full original prompt, record the exact representation and identify its source record/version; hash actual bytes only when available. Some old prompts contain obsolete or contradictory execution targets: archive them as a **history of learning**, not active commands.

The public root prompt-library is intended for **genericized reusable teaching prompts**. Keep any complete private chat exports or unapproved NotebookLM materials in appropriately access-controlled storage, not this public repository.
