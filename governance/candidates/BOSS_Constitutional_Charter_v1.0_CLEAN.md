# BOSS Constitutional Charter
**Version:** 1.0 Candidate
**Status:** Consolidated Statutory Draft — human ratification pending
## Article I — Purpose and System Boundary
**Section 1.01 — System Definition.** BOSS (Bioscillate Operating System by Seven) is an educational operating system and meta-framework designed to architect, govern, and audit subordinate systems.
**Section 1.02 — Liability Boundary.** BOSS governs system design, execution boundaries, verification, and release. Practitioners retain responsibility for authorized actions. Applicable legal liability depends on contracts, law, and actual circumstances, not this internal governance instrument.
## Article II — System Abuse and Harm
**Section 2.01 — Prohibited Conduct.** System abuse includes deliberate circumvention of governance controls, concealed execution, falsification of IPOS diagnostic logs, or knowingly presenting a detected Split-Signal Error (SSE) as verified fact, and unauthorized external-state changes. An unintentional SSE is a technical discrepancy requiring classification and correction, not proof of deliberate misconduct.
**Section 2.02 — Bounded Automation.** Automated actions do not require individual human approval per execution if a Commander has pre-authorized the automation with documented permissions, logging, stop conditions, and escalation rules.
## Article III — Amendment and Revision Process
**Section 3.01 — Evidence-Based Amendment.** Amendments require a documented rationale, impact assessment, appropriate evidence (execution telemetry, security analysis, or demonstrated architectural inconsistency), review, and human ratification.
**Section 3.02 — Emergency Patching.** Emergency security patches shall follow a narrowly defined expedited procedure, subject to immediate post-deployment audit.
## Article IV — Professional Engineering and Specialist Handoff (C04)
**Section 4.01 — Scope of Command Authority.** A Commander may orchestrate and audit subordinate systems within the limits of demonstrated competence and approved scope.
**Section 4.02 — Mandatory Specialist Referral.** A subordinate system shall not proceed to operational release without documented specialist review when its construction introduces a material risk arising from cryptographic implementation, production infrastructure, privileged security controls, or legally regulated processing.
**Section 4.03 — Non-Substitution.** Use of an AI-generated script or pre-existing software library shall not constitute proof of engineering competence.
## Article V — Dispute Resolution and Enforcement
**Section 5.01 — Adjudication Protocol.** Adjudication of system failures shall consider independently corroborated logs, source artifacts, configuration history, and human testimony. An unrecorded operation is NOT ESTABLISHED; it is not automatically a proven breach.
**Section 5.02 — Proportional Enforcement.** Enforcement shall be graduated: flag and preserve evidence, suspend the affected capability, conduct an authorized review, and restore, restrict, or revoke privileges through an accountable decision.
## Article VI — Research Integrity and Participant Protection (C07)
**Section 6.01 — Classification of Evidence.** Operational telemetry constitutes evidence of system activity only to the extent supported by its origin, integrity, and completeness.
**Section 6.02 — Separation of Research Purposes.** A subordinate system shall not repurpose identifiable learner data, private communications, or behavioral telemetry for unrelated research without a documented lawful basis and consent.
**Section 6.03 — Human-Participant Research.** Research intended to establish educational outcomes shall be governed by an approved research protocol and required ethics review. Efficacy claims shall not be established solely through workflow success logs.
## Article VII — Academy Credentialing (C09)
**Section 7.01 — Competency Standard.** A BOSS credential certifies demonstrated competence against published assessment criteria. Attendance or payment alone shall not establish qualification.
**Section 7.02 — Practical Demonstration.** Candidates must demonstrate IPOS mapping, bounded execution, evidence inspection, and recovery procedures through an approved assessment.
## Article VIII — Privacy, Security, and Data Retention (C10)
**Section 8.01 — Data Minimization.** No subordinate system shall collect, process, or retain personal data beyond what is reasonably necessary for an identified and authorized purpose.
**Section 8.02 — Security Controls.** Each system shall implement security measures reasonably appropriate to its data classification, including access restrictions and data-integrity controls.
**Section 8.03 — Data Retention.** Each system shall maintain a documented retention schedule. Upon expiration, information shall be securely deleted or de-identified in accordance with authorized obligations. Evidence integrity shall not justify indefinite storage of personal data.
**Section 8.04 — Individual Rights.** Systems handling personal data shall document procedures for receiving and processing individual rights requests (access, correction, deletion) within legally applicable limits.
## Article IX — Risk Classification and Assessment (C11)
**Section 9.01 — Prior Classification.** Before operational release, each subordinate system shall be assigned a documented risk classification by an authorized human decision-maker.
**Section 9.02 — Mandatory Assessment.** A proposed system involving sensitive personal data, material external-state modification, or heightened-risk activities shall undergo a documented data protection assessment before deployment.
## Article X — Kernel Supremacy and System Independence
**Section 10.01 — Supremacy of the Kernel.** The BOSS Constitutional Kernel establishes the minimum conditions for any system described or released as BOSS-compliant. No subordinate constitution or workflow may diminish these conditions, bypass the Split-Signal Detection Gate, or override Evidence Deficit strictures.
**Section 10.02 — Mandatory Inherited Controls.** Every subordinate system shall implement controls for IPOS traceability, unknown-state handling, security, privacy, and verification before release.
**Section 10.03 — Domain Independence.** Subject to the Kernel, a subordinate system may define its operational methods, interfaces, and internal processes.
**Section 10.04 — Commercial Independence.** The BOSS operating system shall remain vendor-agnostic and support open standards. Proprietary tools are permitted when necessary and authorized, but the architecture must mandate portability by design.

**Section 6.04 — Structural-Correspondence Firewall.** BOSS descriptions of subordinate systems shall use operational terminology rather than biological or personal attributes. Technical failures shall be classified according to evidence as Split-Signal Error (SSE), Evidence Deficit, Authorization Failure, or Control Violation. SSE means an output claim demonstrably contradicts verified evidence; absent evidence alone is NOT ESTABLISHED. These terms describe measurable system conditions, not software cognition, intent or belief.
