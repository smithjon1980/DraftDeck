## Operating thesis
Use the neighborhood **Main Street Copy** copy/print/pack/ship operation as the physical twin of a productive digital facility:

| Physical business | Productive digital infrastructure | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Street address and front door | Domain/DNS/service endpoint | Locator is not a deed or an operating business |
| Building, utilities and controlled access | Compute, storage, network and identity/permission controls | Suitable infrastructure makes work possible |
| Copiers, finishing and packing equipment | Software, APIs and governed workflows | Equipment presence does not prove work occurred |
| Customer file and job ticket | Authorized structured inputs and schema | Submitted data does not automatically authorize production |
| Production/quality bench | Bounded transformation, tests and human inspection | Build success is not accepted output |
| Shipping counter | Authorized exchange and carrier handoff | Label creation is not carrier possession |
| Tracking/delivery receipt | Evidence Chain, receiving boundary and decision record | Custody is not recipient delivery; delivery is not profit |

**Productive Digital Real Estate** means operating resources organized to do useful, repeatable, accountable work. It does not mean ownership of digital land, guaranteed demand or automatic revenue.

## The physical teaching sequence
A hypothetical customer brings an authorized file and job specifications. Main Street Copy would validate the request, prepare/copy/print, inspect quantity and quality, pack and measure a parcel, verify destination and carrier requirements, choose an eligible ground service, obtain explicit human release, hand off to the chosen carrier, and record separately any custody and recipient delivery evidence. An unknown mandatory field causes HOLD, not simulated success.

**UPS Ground and FedEx Ground are examples of alternative outside carrier networks, not a claim of partnership, live price, eligibility or delivery guarantee.** One parcel follows the selected service; labels, acceptance scans, transit updates and delivery receipts represent distinct evidence states. Current rates, cutoff times and eligibility require fresh verification for actual customer transactions.

## The digital teaching sequence
Define a **bounded intake-validation capability first**. The digital input is a document, authorized requester and job specification. The process verifies permitted fields and files; the output is an inspectable readiness/exception report. The system is the governed hosting/storage/identity arrangement. That pilot does NOT establish printed output, pack/ship execution, physical carrier acceptance, actual recipient delivery or profitability.

Later physical and digital stages form a conceptual scope expansion only if authorized and tested. This ensures that technical diagrams cannot silently assert a deployed end-to-end service.

## Distinct teaching lenses
- **IPOS:** Inputs / Process / Outputs / System describes the work and its operating conditions.
- **PARCELS:** Platform / Attachment / Routing / Carriage / Exchange / Language / Service are architectural responsibilities, **not** sequential shipping stations.
- **PRIME:** Package / Route / Inspect / Move / Establish Delivery governs bounded movement and evidence; human authority remains distinct.
- **SEVEN:** Survey / Evaluate / Validate / Execute / Normalize governs the wider consulting/operational inquiry.
- **Evidence Chain:** source identity → permitted event → artifact → scoped tests → human acceptance; neither a pass badge nor a shipment label proves downstream outcomes.
