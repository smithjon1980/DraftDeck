# Main Street Copy — current BOSS reference business (candidate doctrine)

## Scope, status and supersession
As explicitly directed by the project owner, **Main Street Copy** is the current fictional neighborhood copy, print, pack and ship business for contemporary BOSS lessons and the Episode 01 visual story. It supersedes the older convenience-retail reference scenario. Historical copy and images remain evidence, not current creative authority. The illustrated shop and workflow are instructional **simulations**, not verified deployments or a shipping-carrier partnership.

## Physical business case
A customer brings a file and request to Main Street Copy. An employee creates a bounded job ticket covering file acceptance, page count, quantity, single/double sided, finish, recipient/delivery location and required timeline. If the ticket is incomplete, **HOLD** before executing. After quote/acceptance and explicit operator authorization, the shop copies/prints, inspects against the ticket, packs/protects and measures the parcel, verifies the address, eligible service and carrier terms, then labels and hands it to a selected carrier. The shop retains source, inspection, handoff and tracking evidence. A printed label is *not* proof of carrier possession; a carrier acceptance scan is *not* proof of delivery. Track events and delivery confirmation independently.

## Visual storytelling spine
Main Street Copy supplies the *business scene*. Show an isometric or cutaway shop on a pure white drafting background with physically intelligible distinct stations:
1. **INTAKE / SPEC**: counter, customer's files, job ticket, dimensions and requirements.
2. **COPY / PRINT**: print engine, job settings and actual completed pages.
3. **INSPECT**: a visible quality-check decision; mismatched count/address/format causes HOLD.
4. **PACK**: protective wrap, carton, scale and recipient label proof.
5. **SHIP / ROUTE**: two alternative appropriately qualified Ground carrier routes (UPS Ground or FedEx Ground), not simultaneous transport for the same single parcel. The human compares eligible terms, cutoff, cost and destination, then selects and authorizes a service.
6. **TRACK / RECEIPT**: transfer-of-custody record, subsequent carrier status, and recipient delivery evidence; unresolved evidence stays UNKNOWN.

## Teaching mappings (keep them distinct)
- **Copy → Pack → Ship** is the accessible real-world business analogy, not an exhaustive audited transaction model.
- **PARCELS** maps architecture roles: **Platform / Attachment / Routing / Carriage / Exchange / Language / Service**. These are responsibilities, **not** sequential shop transaction stages.
- **PRIME** maps a governed work handoff: **Package / Route / Inspect / Move / Establish Delivery**. Verification and human release authority govern transitions; a missing input or failed inspection causes HOLD.
- The complete physical operation has stages of intake/specification, production, quality inspection, packing, carrier selection/handoff, and tracking/delivery evidence. These may be explained using the PRIME and PARCELS lenses but must never be falsely presented as identical acronyms or a mandatory one-to-one mapping.
- **UPS Ground / FedEx Ground** serve as two real-world shipping-system examples: package preparation, label/addressing, service selection, handoff, carrier sorting and transport, scan/status updates, delivery evidence. Prices, delivery estimates, shipping restrictions, service eligibility and pickup/dropoff cutoffs vary and require current confirmation before a factual customer quote or promise. No claim that Main Street Copy is a real authorized shipper or that delivery is guaranteed.
