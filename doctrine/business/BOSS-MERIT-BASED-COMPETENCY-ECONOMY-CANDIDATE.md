# BOSS Business Model — Merit-Based Competency Economy
## Commercial Governance Amendment · Candidate v0.1 · 11 October 2026

**Status:** CANDIDATE — NOT RATIFIED OR DEPLOYED  
**Parent:** BOSS Business Architecture & Commercialization Blueprint v3.2 (candidate)  
**Governing principle:** POWER = GOVERNED FLOW  
**Commercial principle:** VALUE MUST PRECEDE RECRUITMENT  
**Public communication principle:** DEMONSTRATION OVER DECLARATION

### 1. Purpose and boundaries
BOSS exists to teach transferable systems literacy, tool judgment, structured design, independent verification, and accountable human decision-making. Its commercial success must not depend on learners recruiting others. BOSS will demonstrate its distinction through observable policies, outcomes, and records rather than attacks on competing business models.

This amendment adds business-model rules to the existing blueprint; it does not declare a production platform, legal compliance, validated learning outcomes, or ratification. Earlier documents are preserved.

### 2. The merit-based competency economy
**Knowledge creates capability. Capability creates value. Value creates opportunity.**

Advancement is earned by demonstrated competencies, not enrollment spend, recruiting volume, social proximity, or promotional performance.

Proposed learning progression: Learner → Practitioner → Verified Builder → Instructor / Independent Creator. These are candidate educational designations, not professional licenses or automatic claims of software engineering qualifications. Instructors must separately demonstrate teaching competence; specialist tasks require appropriately qualified review.

Evaluation must use published rubrics, accessible assessment routes, inspectable work products, qualified review, and a fair appeal or reassessment procedure. Equal access and rigorous standards are compatible. Merit is evidence of demonstrated ability, not a claim about personal worth.

### 3. Commercial safeguards
1. **No multi-level referral commissions.** No payment for recruits-of-recruits, downlines, binary legs, or recruitment volume.
2. **No pay-to-qualify compensation.** Purchasing a course, membership, kit, or higher tier cannot by itself unlock earning eligibility or credential status.
3. **Education must stand alone.** A learner receives substantive value even if they never recommend or recruit anyone.
4. **Compensate deliverable value.** Pay educators for instruction, mentors for documented mentorship, creators for licensed work, and builders for accepted deliverables under transparent contracts.
5. **Optional single-tier referrals only if later approved.** Any referral program requires disclosure, documented genuine sales, reasonable refund handling, no recruitment-chain rewards, and separate review.
6. **No earnings promises.** Disclose material costs, limitations, and actual substantiated results; distinguish illustrative examples from observed outcomes.
7. **No forced dependence.** Learners retain appropriately licensed, exportable work and transferable knowledge; do not market essential competency as permanently gated behind upsells.

### 4. Transparent pricing and compensation
Before payment, learners should see curriculum scope, total known charges, renewal terms, refund terms, assessment requirements, and any optional infrastructure expenses.

Every paid contributor should receive an understandable calculation statement: eligible work or sale, agreed rate, adjustments, refunds, period, and payout. An independent reviewer should be able to recalculate the amount without interpreting opaque branch or rank rules.

### 5. Demonstration over declaration
BOSS should publish inspectable evidence rather than demand agreement with marketing claims. The platform is both a teaching environment and a documented case study of the method used to construct it.

Evidence may include design decisions, source revisions, controlled tests, failed experiments, corrections, deployment receipts, and learner assessments with appropriate privacy protections.

**Evidence separation:** a committed design is not a deployed service; a network event is not proof of remote execution; a stored artifact is not proof of visual correctness; an attendance record is not proof of competence.

### 6. Measurement and accountability
Measure and publish, with clear denominators, cohort dates, definitions, and caveats where appropriate:
- enrollment and total learner cost;
- completion and assessment participation;
- independently evaluated competency attainment;
- project portability and successful reproduction outside BOSS;
- learner feedback and accessible appeals;
- retention, refunds, acquisition cost, and sustainable unit economics.

Do not substitute enrollment, referral counts, badges, or testimonials for evidence of learning. Do not publish identifiable learner artifacts or research data without appropriate authority, consent, and protection. Educational efficacy claims require distinct valid evaluation, not inference from operational logs alone.

### 7. Customer acquisition model
**Attract through useful public instruction → teach a genuine capability → enable an independently inspectable artifact → verify learning → invite an optional next step.**

A learner may complete a program and leave without losing recognition of earned competencies. Alumni may recommend BOSS voluntarily, but referral activity must not affect grades, credentials, access to teachers, or educational status.

### 8. Non-adversarial positioning
BOSS will not define itself publicly by hostility toward network marketing, affiliates, political identities, or other educational providers. Individuals may draw comparisons from BOSS's observable policies. BOSS representatives should answer with the documented rules and evidence, while acknowledging limitations.

Suggested language: **“At BOSS, you do not advance by recruiting people. You advance by demonstrating what you understand, what you can build, and how you verified it.”**

### 9. Proposed release gates and unknowns
**Gate C1 — Policy:** human approval of compensation, refund, privacy, assessment, and appeals policies.  
**Gate C2 — Product:** usable curriculum, accessible rubrics, exportable artifacts, and independent assessment pilots.  
**Gate C3 — Economics:** documented pricing, costs, refund experience, and viable acquisition economics without recruitment-dependent earnings.  
**Gate C4 — Claims:** evidence review before publishing learner success, income, or career claims.  
**Gate C5 — Audit:** periodic checks for drift toward recruiting incentives, pay-to-qualify structures, or opaque commissions.

**Current state:** All gates NOT ESTABLISHED until supporting records and accountable human sign-off are available.

### 10. Constitutional tests
**Learner independence test:** If BOSS accepted no new students tomorrow, would existing learners still possess valuable, independently usable and demonstrably earned capabilities?

**Compensation intelligibility test:** Could a contributor independently calculate each payment from disclosed terms and an itemized statement?

**Evidence test:** Can an informed outsider distinguish what was promised, built, tested, verified, and not established?

Failure of any test requires correction before the relevant commercial claim is promoted.
