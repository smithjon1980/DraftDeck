# BOSS doctrine index — present scope and historical record

Do not treat every document under doctrine/ as simultaneously current. This repository contains evolving, pre-release architecture as well as historical demonstration material. Git history preserves previous drafts; conflicting old language must not silently override later explicit project decisions.

## Current design and release reading order
1. ../AGENTS.md — current operating constraints and human authority.
2. ../design-system/boss-design-contract.json — current design-system candidate and explicit known unknowns.
3. composition-standard.md — BOSS visual standard candidate.
4. production-contract.md — authored-source versus renderer/output custody.
5. reference-render-protocol.md — reference and layer preservation.
6. release-qa.md — required evidence, scope, and human release boundary.
7. evidence-verification-architecture.md and bounded-autonomy-execution-envelope.md — deeper evidence and control-plane principles, subject to their own status and scope.

## Historical / domain-specific material
Other doctrine documents, including prior logistics and parcel-themed versions, can remain to preserve work and reproductions. They are not newly approved BOSS visual or teaching ontology merely by existing under this folder. Pending architecture proposals must be adjudicated separately; a design-system patch does not silently ratify, repeal, or migrate the entire BOSS architecture.

## Authority
Explicit current human decisions > approved current scoped contract > current source/implementation mappings > historical experiments. A review candidate is not an approved release. The design-system contract remains a candidate until the original logo source and representative render are verified and accepted.
