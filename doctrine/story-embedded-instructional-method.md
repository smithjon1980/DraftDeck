## 1. Constitutional rule

Instruction begins with a learner's mission and learning envelope. Within that envelope, BOSS should teach through an unfolding professional story that creates a reason to learn, not a disconnected lecture. **The story creates the need to learn; instruction develops capability; evidence demonstrates whether it works.** Storytelling does not override accuracy, accessibility, learner needs, or human authority.

## 2. Story-embedded instructional cycle

**Story → Problem → Principle → Application → Evidence → Story continues.**

- **Story:** Place the learner in a realistic decision context.
- **Problem:** Surface a specific uncertainty, conflict, or responsibility.
- **Principle:** Explain the relevant concept when it becomes useful.
- **Application:** Demonstrate a bounded, executable action.
- **Evidence:** Show how success, failure, and uncertainty are established.
- **Story continues:** Carry consequences into the next situation.

The cycle may span multiple paragraphs and slides; do not force all six stages into every paragraph. Preserve manuscript paragraph-to-slide anchors and separately identify assessment/supporting slides.

## 3. Narrative architecture and balance

- **Section One:** approximately **70% narrative / 30% explicit instruction**, an editorial target rather than a mechanical word-count gate.
- **Section Two:** progressively more operator decisions, guided practice, and evidence-based assessments, subject to reconciliation with its authoritative manuscript.
- **Narrative baseline:** fictional Texas corporate campus → executive → architect → developer → Main Street Copy → carrier → receiving office.

## 4. Operator-discipline thread

Use the uploaded motivational video's instructional *ideas* as inspiration for original teaching, without copying narration or presenting its unverified military/neuroscience claims as facts:

1. **Reduce the mission:** identify the next bounded executable step.
2. **Rehearse the workflow:** anticipate exceptions, dependencies, and stopping conditions.
3. **Issue precise commands:** replace vague requests with clear instructions and acceptance criteria.
4. **Regain operational control:** pause uncontrolled execution, inspect evidence, correct, and resume from a verified state.

These are pedagogical behaviors, not new proprietary acronyms. The fourth is an operational analogy, not a replacement for personal stress-management instruction.

## 5. HAVE terminology and authority

**HAVE = Human Authority Verified Evidence.** This definition supersedes earlier drafts reading “Human Authority, Verified Execution.” Do not use “Humans in the Lead” as BOSS branding. Execution is not authorization; output is not proof. Specialized production tools execute; validation systems test; evidence records; humans approve consequential editorial and release decisions.

Legacy materials containing superseded terminology must be flagged for controlled migration; this specification does **not** silently modify historical approved artifacts.

## 6. Creative brief and component contract

Every manuscript-anchored creative brief must declare:
- manuscript ID, paragraph anchor, revision and approval status;
- learner mission, objective, and evidence of understanding;
- primary slide family (Editorial, Process, Consulting, Architecture, Evidence, Comparison, Interactive);
- story beat and SEIM cycle stage(s);
- existing component IDs and any proposed library additions;
- ART brief and STORY brief as independently reviewable artifacts;
- vector CAD/ISO geometry, editable native text, CMYK/white design constraints, and any accessibility requirements;
- acceptance criteria, provenance, and explicit human approval gates.

Approved creative excellence establishes the visual target; the component library makes it reproducible. New components require qualification and human approval rather than suppressing stronger artwork.

## 7. Production boundaries

**Manuscript → classification → creative brief → human brief approval → master reference → specialized image production (ComfyUI where configured) → separate ART/STORY approvals → editable composition → automated verification → human release.**

ChatGPT supervises and reports; it does not silently replace specialized production functions. GitHub Actions orchestrates/validates, not image-generates. No contact sheets in lieu of full-resolution individual review. SVG/CAD geometry remains vector-based; instructional text remains editable. A foundation approval is not publication approval.

## 8. Qualification gates

A section qualifies only when:
1. Each teaching objective is anchored to an approved or explicitly candidate manuscript passage.
2. The learner encounters an intelligible problem and can identify the next action.
3. The taught principle is applied to the story, with observable verification or a clearly labeled unknown.
4. The story remains coherent and the learner can transfer the behavior to an exercise.
5. Slide classification and components are traceable; artwork and story approvals remain distinct.
6. HAVE terminology and human authority are correct; no deprecated branding leaks into release artifacts.
7. Existing approval statuses, evidence custody, and manuscript-to-slide mappings remain intact.

**Automated checks may validate structure and traceability; human editorial review judges narrative and visual quality.**
