### Proposed art layer (separate approval)

**Subject:** One capable adult working professional in a realistically lived-in modern office in the waning hours of Friday. They have just completed routine tasks: organized messages, printed marked-up documents, a packed weekly planner, laptop with non-legible generic inbox tiles, meeting materials and a closed folder.

**Crucial story cue:** Beyond those completed tasks, a single large priority project remains in an obviously *unchanged* condition: untouched architectural/project proposal folder, clean unopened strategic planning board or a carefully staged document with no review marks. This contrast must be visible without relying on tiny screen text or invented numerical KPI dashboards.

**Camera:** Credible editorial environmental portrait; human at three-quarter angle, expressive but not theatrical; main photograph receives the dominant area and leaves safe negative space outside the photo for independently editable headlines. Natural early evening window light; material fidelity on paper, desk, glass and fabric. Older professional audience cues without stereotype.

**Reference-derived visual craft:** High-end photorealistic publishing spreads, brilliant white ground, editorial serif/cyan italic contrast, fine CAD construction hairlines in the non-photographic region, deliberate CMYK bars and registration marks; no cartoon or vector humans.

**Excluded from the photo:** Boardroom group reviewing an already built system, full-screen AI dashboard, futuristic hologram, robot, fake paragraph text, fabricated graphs, decorative sparks, unreadable pseudo-labels, self-congratulatory verification badges.
