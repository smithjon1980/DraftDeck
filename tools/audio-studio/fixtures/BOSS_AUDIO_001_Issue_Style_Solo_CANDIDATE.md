What makes an empty warehouse productive? Is an address enough? Or must someone decide which shipments may enter, what work is permitted, and how delivery will be checked?

Picture a package at the loading dock. The door is open. Does that prove the package belongs there? Certainly not. The operator needs a destination, permission to handle it, and a record of what happens.

Now consider digital infrastructure. Windows eleven is the operator's control station. Ubuntu can serve as a remote receiving and execution facility. Tailscale provides private connectivity. Secure Shell, or SSH, authenticates access. But access alone does not prove complete security or permission to perform every task.

Our first digital work package is modest. We identify an exact DraftDeck source revision, authorize a limited check, execute it, and collect the evidence. A matching checksum establishes corresponding file bytes—not that every claim is true. A passing policy check establishes the tested condition—not permission to release the result.

PRIME names the movement: Package, Route, Inspect, Move, Establish Delivery. PARCELS locates responsibilities. Human authority governs acceptance.

Before you proceed, ask: What did we receive? What did we verify? Who may approve delivery?

Manual First. Automated Second. Governed Always.
