Imagine renting an empty warehouse. You receive an address, a key, and a loading dock, but that does not mean you have a productive operation. Before any shipment moves, the operator must define who can enter, what work is permitted, and how delivery will be verified.

BOSS applies the same discipline to digital infrastructure. Windows eleven serves as the operator's control station. An Ubuntu server can become a remote receiving and execution facility. Tailscale provides private connectivity, while SSH supports authenticated access. Neither connection alone proves that a task is authorized or that the entire facility is secure.

Our first digital work package is deliberately small. We identify an exact source revision, specify a limited operation, execute it, and collect the returned evidence. A matching file checksum establishes byte correspondence, not the accuracy of every claim. A passing policy check establishes the tested condition, not unrestricted permission.

PRIME means Package, Route, Inspect, Move, and Establish Delivery. PARCELS identifies where operational responsibilities reside. Human authority governs acceptance across the whole process.

Manual First. Automated Second. Governed Always. Execution is mechanics. Verified delivery requires inspection and accountable acceptance.
