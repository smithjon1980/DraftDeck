## Instructional lesson
The research record documents **what discipline looks like in choices**, not just in statements: exclude a technique that failed this particular visual workflow, separate creative generation from script manufacturing, require human approval, and measure whether a tool meets the assignment. Repeated practice without credible feedback could automate error.
