## Questions for each entry
1. What was the **constraint**?
2. What did Gemini **actually contribute**?
3. What did the **human decide**?
4. What was **rejected or revised**?
5. What actually changed in GitHub or a production system?
6. What evidence is still missing?
7. How would a learner repeat the useful process without inheriting unsupported claims?

**Teaching priority:** The archive is a lens into judgment and practice. The purpose is to teach discipline through real constrained choices, not to glorify the tools or accumulate prompts/responses for their own sake.
