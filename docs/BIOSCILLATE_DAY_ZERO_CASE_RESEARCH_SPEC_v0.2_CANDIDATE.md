## 1. Purpose and controlling decisions

Create **one 250-substantive-paragraph** Day Zero instructional manuscript that behaves as a recurring, original consulting case simulator, not as 250 unrelated definitions or an unstructured sales pitch. The Main Street Copy scenario supplies continuity: a human consultant interviews an owner, inspects order evidence, investigates operational constraints, compares alternatives, requests authorization, and evaluates outcomes. Case dialogue, quote callouts, financial exhibits, citations, annotations, tables, reflection questions, exercises, and Perplexity prompts **must not count** as numbered instructional paragraphs. Avoid numbered exercise-only entries presented as prose. Count *only* substantive narrative/instructional paragraphs labelled `001.` through `250.` outside supporting apparatus.

The previous erroneous spoken "Paragraph 001" about afternoon sun, school rushes, pricing exceptions, or a printer-purchase walkthrough is **not** Candidate 008 Paragraph 001. It is ineligible as an evidence anchor and must never be substituted for the source text. All prompts reproduce the exact approved-source candidate paragraph **verbatim**, including punctuation and emphasis; no reconstruction from voice, assistant memory, or summary is allowed.

## 3. Established doctrine — one method, not three competing frameworks

**SEVEN:** **Survey → Evaluate → Validate → Execute → Normalize** governs the full consulting and operating inquiry. First instructional anchor is **P016**, after a real client problem has been established.

**PRIME:** **Package → Route → Inspect → Move → Establish Delivery** governs bounded movement, handoffs and delivery evidence. First instructional anchor is **P052**, when the learner follows one order.

**PARCELS:** **Platform → Attachment → Routing → Carriage → Exchange → Language → Service** locates structural responsibilities and dominant failure modes; it is **not** a sequence of seven shipping stations. First instructional anchor is **P076**, when the learner classifies repeated defects.

HAVE preserves explicit human authority, scoped verification and release decision boundaries. IPOS and the Evidence Chain are separate, complementary teaching lenses. Consulting case interviews from established firms may inform *generic instructional techniques*, but do not copy proprietary case texts or replace SEVEN.

## 4. Instructional composition

Build a continuous progression in which case stakes and evidence become more complex. Follow the seven-stage cycle **inside SEVEN**, not as a new named discovery methodology: presenting problem; clarifying interview; competing explanations; source and operational evidence; hypothesis test; bounded recommendation; authorized pilot and verified outcome.

Include short, original scripted client-consultant dialogues at meaningful plot turns, particularly discovery, objection handling, and the difference between recommendation and authorization. These are labeled `CASE SCENE` or `SCRIPTED DIALOGUE`, never `NNN.` prose paragraphs. Fictional names/events are pedagogical devices, not independently sourced case evidence. Exhibits distinguish **illustrative invented numbers** from **verified public data**.

## 5. Per-paragraph research contract — mandatory

Maintain **exactly one independently copyable Perplexity Pro research packet per paragraph P001–P250**. Research packets are alongside, but excluded from, the 250 narrative paragraph count. The packet is self-contained so it can be pasted into Perplexity *without relying on chat history*.

Each packet SHALL include:
1. Canonical identifier PNNN and source-candidate version.
2. The **entire exact paragraph text word for word**, from `NNN.` through its final character, as the cited claim context (not merely its first sentence or a paraphrase).
3. A paragraph-specific, action-oriented research question; ask what evidence would **support, limit or contradict** the concrete principle raised by the paragraph. Reusable instructions may follow, but the context/question must change with the actual paragraph.
4. Explicit request to locate **exactly three distinct, independently identifiable and credible sources**, preferentially original research, professional standards, peer-reviewed studies, authoritative institutional materials, or transparent primary studies. If fewer than three qualify, state the shortage; never fabricate.
5. For every source: publisher/author, full title, publication date and evidence period, original URL/DOI, relevant passage/page/section, methods and scope, limitations, applicable geographic or industry setting, and the precise manuscript claim it does or does not substantiate.
6. An exact-copy annotated version of the paragraph with only **parenthetical citation markers `(C1)`, `(C2)`, `(C3)`** added after genuinely supported claims; a fenced code block may be used for copy-and-paste formatting. Preserve wording, order and punctuation. Never cite fictional dialogue as a documented event.
7. A three-source bibliography, claim-to-source assessment (`DIRECT SUPPORT`, `PARTIAL SUPPORT`, `CONTRADICTION`, `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`), flagged unsupported claims, and separately proposed editorial changes.
8. `RESEARCH PENDING / SOURCE VERIFICATION PENDING / HUMAN EDITORIAL APPROVAL PENDING` until source passages have been inspected by an authorized reviewer. Perplexity output is discovery, not independent verification. Other models' agreement does not establish validity.

**Citation placeholders are not verified citations.** The three slots `(C1) PENDING`, `(C2) PENDING`, `(C3) PENDING` may appear adjacent to the corresponding research packet, but the manuscript may not present them as authoritative references before claim-to-source inspection.
