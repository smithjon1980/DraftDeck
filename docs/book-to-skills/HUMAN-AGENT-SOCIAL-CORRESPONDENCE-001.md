# BOSS Social Correspondence Contract — Human ↔ Agent
Status: SPECIFIED_CANDIDATE / NOT_TESTED | Cycle 04 redesign | Applies to the 25 extracted Hormozi research skills

## Central idea
A skill is not simply an instruction for an AI to execute. It is a **paired capability** with one human side and one agent side. The intended result is a reliable one-to-one handoff: the human supplies lived operational context, meaning, priorities and legitimate authority; the agent structures, compares, drafts, tests and records evidence. Neither substitutes for the other. Do not anthropomorphize the agent or imply equal legal/moral status. Social correspondence describes reciprocal *functional* responsibilities and understandable interaction, not equality of authority.

## Five interaction moments
1. **Opening feed hook / invitation:** Start with a specific human problem in the business's own words. Ask a non-manipulative question; never assume consent or present an invented client story as fact.
2. **Human story and work context:** Capture business setting, customers' tasks, constraints, emotional friction and observed failures with permission and minimum necessary data.
3. **Agent reflection and structural translation:** Echo the observed need, separate fact from inference, show a compact workflow or options, and explicitly ask the human what is incorrect.
4. **Mutual verification and decision:** Agent supplies evidence, limitations, alternatives and a test. Human confirms intent and owns all reserved decisions. If disagreement or evidence gap, preserve both and mark HOLD.
5. **Relationship continuity:** Return outcome, next step and correction path to the human. Preserve feedback and rejected hypotheses for the next iteration.

## Required SKILL interaction contract
Each skill SHALL declare:
- `human_intent`: what human/business problem initiates this work.
- `human_inputs`: what the operator directly knows, controls or explicitly authorizes.
- `agent_responsibility`: bounded analytic/design work, never reserved human judgment.
- `dialogue_opening`: short, plain-language conversation opener, not compulsory sales copy.
- `clarification_trigger`: evidence gap, ambiguous operational meaning or conflicting intent.
- `human_decision_checkpoint`: explicit point where the agent stops for review.
- `observable_proof`: outcome and evidence status; do not claim business results based on mock or source-only data.
- `repair_path`: correction, rollback/HOLD, preserved competing explanations, next iteration.
- `provenance_contract`: source ID, facts/inferences, trust, L0–L3 chain if established.
- `authority_boundary`: G1–G5, open seams and no production authorization.
This contract is additional to Goal / Proof / Steps / Boundary, not a substitute.

## Social correspondence vs structural correspondence
- Structural correspondence asks, "Does a pattern in a source map to a BOSS process?"
- Social correspondence asks, "Can the human and agent correctly understand, negotiate, observe, and repair that process together?"
- Commercial correspondence asks, "Does the result solve a buyer's problem with sustainable economics?"
A skill is not relationship-ready merely because it has a good sales hook or valid code.
