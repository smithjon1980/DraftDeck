# BOSS Collaboration and Milestone Protocol

Version: v1.1 candidate protocol.
Goal: develop research practice in learning design and governed information work while improving BOSS.

## Shared responsibilities
Jonathan sets intent, explains and challenges the design rationale, reviews the learner experience and records what he can defend independently. The assistant organizes source material, builds authorized drafts, traces claims to evidence, exposes unresolved conditions and proposes the next bounded task. Record AI assistance separately from the author's independently demonstrated reasoning.

Keep learner teaching, instructor keys, project research and author development distinct. Research guidance informs the project without silently changing doctrine or learner qualification rules.

## Milestone contract
Use six milestones: design coherence; research grounding; study design; evidence analysis; bounded contribution; independent critique.

For each, record its question, current state, required checks, evidence references, unresolved checks and one next action in milestone-state.json. Use the enumerated states not_started, drafted, tested and independently_reviewed.

DRAFTED requires a usable deliverable and an identifiable source basis. TESTED requires the author to explain the reasoning, the relevant proposition or method to be challenged with appropriate evidence, and the response to material problems to be recorded. INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED requires critique from a relevant independent person and a documented response. Define the actual gate checks for each milestone before judging completion; these generic descriptions do not substitute for milestone-specific evidence.

Attach a source and reason to every verified check. Mark missing evidence pending. Reopen a gate when a revision invalidates earlier evidence, preserving the earlier record. A reviewer or learner result may challenge a conclusion without erasing the work that produced it.

Report proximity as verified checks out of the explicitly defined checks for the next gate, followed by the missing evidence. Never translate that count into a percentage of PhD completion, a general intelligence score or a degree-equivalence claim. A smaller checklist does not imply a smaller intellectual gap.

## Working-session protocol
At the start: identify the current milestone, source/version, intended deliverable and acceptance evidence. Reuse established context and ask only for required missing decisions.

During work: distinguish proposals, built artifacts, checked properties and results established for a particular use. Explain consequential reasoning in the BOSS Guided Reasoning Format. Use question, case, pressure test, standard, worked case, operator move and boundary as appropriate to the artifact.

At the close: summarize changes with artifact links; state which checks were performed; record new evidence, remaining questions and the next bounded action. Update the milestone record only from evidence actually reviewed. Do not imply background work continues after a turn or that an automation has run merely because it was scheduled.

## Daily briefing contract
Schedule: 8 PM America/Chicago, selected by Jonathan. Automation configuration is recorded separately after successful creation.

Prepare a concise read-only briefing containing:
- What changed since the preceding briefing, with source references; if there is no accessible prior briefing, identify the comparison window used.
- Current milestone state and verified/required checks for the next gate.
- The most consequential evidence gap or blocker, including stale or inaccessible state.
- One next bounded action and what would establish its completion.
- Any required decision from Jonathan; omit this item when none is needed.

Use fresh GitHub reads, the milestone record and available project conversation evidence. Distinguish a changed file from a demonstrated capability. Report no material change plainly; do not fabricate daily progress. If evidence cannot be accessed, disclose that limit and avoid estimated advancement. A briefing does not itself change milestone states or approve release.

## Versioned structure
Keep this protocol, boss-development-and-evidence-plan.md, milestone-state.json, milestone-state.schema.json and daily-briefing-config.json under training/research/. Store versioned skill definitions under training/research/skills/. The structured records can later support a program that displays evidence, required checks and briefing history; the current implementation is the protocol, skills and scheduled briefing, not a finished application.

## Release boundaries
The Module 01 review is candidate material. Audio/PDF correspondence and learner outcomes remain to be checked. Existing task scoring rules remain provisional. Original research contribution and degree-equivalent competence are not established by artifact completion.

## Daily research-design scorecard — added 2026-10-09

Author-selected scope: one paper, one hypothesis. Process-first AI education may improve beginners' transfer to unfamiliar AI tools compared with product-first instruction. Treat this as untested. Workflow reliability is not a separate study track in this paper.

Include this scorecard in each daily brief alongside existing milestone gates:

| Category | Maximum points |
|---|---:|
| Alignment with the educational purpose | 20 |
| Focused, bounded research question | 15 |
| Fair comparison | 25 |
| Measurable transfer assessment | 25 |
| Reproducibility and claim discipline | 15 |
| Total | 100 |

This is a project-specific proposed design rubric, not a validated academic instrument, publication probability, learner grade or measure of effectiveness. The earlier conversational 7/10 and 9/10 judgments were not calculated from it; do not convert them into 70/100 and 90/100 baselines.

Before awarding numerical points, define and version item-level criteria, point allocations and acceptable evidence. Until then report each category as unassessed; no current numerical total is established. Do not infer points from file count, effort or an assistant's completion report.

Each brief reports:
- Rubric version and exact inspected source revisions.
- Earned/max points by category, linked evidence and the reason for each awarded item; unassessed points separately.
- Total verified points out of 100 only once all categories have been assessed. Before full assessment report verified points, assessed maximum and unassessed maximum without extrapolating a total.
- Change from the prior evidence-backed assessment, or no comparable baseline.
- Largest remaining gap and one bounded action, with the artifact or check needed to close it.
- Reopened criteria when a revision invalidates previous evidence; preserve assessment history.

Keep design readiness distinct from learner outcomes. A fully met rubric would mean the defined design criteria are satisfied, not that BOSS works better, that a paper will be accepted, or that independent methods review is unnecessary.

Initial gap to close: draft a versioned, item-level scoring rubric and define the primary transfer outcome, recommended as independently verified task completion on an unfamiliar tool. Specify fair lesson time and practice, prior-experience handling, assessment tasks neither group rehearses, scoring, sampling and analysis before outcome collection. Do not claim these study-design tasks are completed by adding this briefing requirement.

The existing 8 PM America/Chicago schedule is unchanged. This protocol amendment does not create, update or verify an automation.
