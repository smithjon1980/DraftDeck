# Module 01 Assessor Calibration

**Version:** v1.0 candidate assessment package.
**Status:** Proposed pilot instruments; no learner outcomes or validated cut scores are claimed.

## Purpose and evidence status
The following responses are constructed scoring anchors, not actual learner results.
Calibration prepares assessors; it does not by itself establish reliability in the pilot cohort.

## Procedure
1. Give assessors the learner packet, R2 and rubric.
2. Each assessor independently scores the specimens below and records cited evidence before consulting expected interpretations.
3. Compare criterion scores, critical-error flags and task dispositions.
4. Discuss disagreements against anchors; revise ambiguous wording/version before pilot administration.
5. Independently rescore a fresh specimen after revisions. Preserve first ratings separately.
Do not use reconciled scores to report independent agreement.
