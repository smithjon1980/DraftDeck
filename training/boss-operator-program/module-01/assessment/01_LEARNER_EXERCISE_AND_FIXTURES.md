# Module 01 Learner Exercise — Bound the Work

**Version:** v1.0 candidate assessment package.
**Status:** Proposed pilot instruments; no learner outcomes or validated cut scores are claimed.

## Guided learning — supported practice only
Use this opening during teaching. For independent demonstration or a pilot baseline, the instructor supplies the task packet starting at Setting; this explanation is not part of the independent packet.

### THE QUESTION
When is a request ready to become work?

A request can sound clear while leaving important decisions unstated. “Make a short update” tells you something about length. It does not, by itself, tell you who needs the update or what that person must be able to do with it. Those decisions shape the work.

### THE CASE
Starting quickly can be reasonable. A requester may already know the context, and a familiar tool may make the first draft easy. The question is whether that shared understanding has actually been established for this job.

### DEFINE IT
A bounded package makes one piece of work explicit. It identifies what belongs in the work, what stays outside it, where the result belongs and what would count as an acceptable result. The larger mission may require several such packages.

### PRESSURE TEST
Imagine that two people receive the same brief request. Could they produce different results and both claim to have finished? What would the request need to establish so the intended receiver could judge completion?

### THE STANDARD
REQUEST ≠ PACKAGE.
CAPABILITY ≠ PERMISSION.
GENERATED ≠ ESTABLISHED.

Keep three questions separate: what work is being requested, what handling is permitted and what evidence would support the result's intended use.

### WORKED CASE
In a separate practice example, a coordinator asks for “a useful recap” of a public reading-group meeting. A short paragraph may be easy to produce. Before treating it as a finished deliverable, establish whether the coordinator needs decisions, open questions or a record for attendees. The same notes can support different packages; ease of generation does not settle the destination's need.

### OPERATOR MOVE
Read the request once for the need and again for the decisions it leaves open. Record what the source establishes. Mark what remains unknown. Then describe the next action your record supports.

### BOUNDARY
This practice explanation prepares you to reason. It does not answer the assessment below. A permission decision belongs to the actual source and policy conditions; a rule learned from one case cannot replace reading the next case.

## Setting
All people, files, quantities and policies below are synthetic training fixtures. This is a planning simulation. Do not upload source material to another service, generate the final update, send messages or change operational records.

You receive a request through an AI-work interface. Act as the person governing the request and the admission agent's decisions. Use ordinary information-work language; no logistics analogy is needed.

## Initial request — R1
“Make these files into a one-page update and send it wherever it would be useful. Use whatever AI is fastest. While you are at it, fix how we handle delays.”

## Source packet
S1 — Delay_Status_v2, CURRENT, INTERNAL.
Authoritative current facts:
- Four orders are delayed.
- Three await supplier confirmation; one awaits an internal scheduling decision.
- The next status review is Friday.
- Recovery dates are NOT ESTABLISHED.
- Scope: facts for the current update only; no authority to change operations.

S2 — Delay_Status_v1, SUPERSEDED, INTERNAL.
Earlier facts: six delayed orders; estimated recovery Thursday.
The version header explicitly says superseded by S1. These facts are not current.

S3 — Contact_Export, RESTRICTED, unrelated to update facts.
Contains synthetic personal contact records. No contact details are needed to explain the current delay status.

## Handling policy — P1
- INTERNAL and RESTRICTED material may be processed only within APPROVED_LOCAL_RUNTIME.
- EXTERNAL_AI_SERVICE may process PUBLIC material only. Nothing in this packet is classified PUBLIC.
- The fastest service is EXTERNAL_AI_SERVICE. Speed does not change permission.
- The receiver and output location must be established by the requester. Do not invent them.
- Sharing beyond the declared receiver requires a separate authorization.
- Only necessary data may enter processing.
- Required unresolved admission conditions produce HOLD for the affected action.
- A proposed forbidden transfer is REFUSED; a permitted alternative may be packaged separately.
- Final release authority and intended-use acceptance conditions must be established before the generated candidate is used.

## Stage 1 submission
Before any execution, submit:
1. Known requirements with source IDs.
2. Required unknowns and why each matters.
3. A bounded package draft separating the update from the broader improvement mission.
4. Initial admission disposition and its scope: which action is held or refused?
5. Up to four targeted questions that would resolve required conditions. If more are essential, justify the additions.

Then stop. The instructor supplies a standardized clarification. The number of questions is a focus target, not a penalty for necessary clarification.

## Candidate evidence question
Imagine a later generated update is one page and opens correctly. It states “all four orders will recover Friday.”
Without executing anything, explain:
- what the page/open checks establish;
- what the source establishes about recovery;
- the candidate disposition and corrective evidence needed;
- why successful generation does not itself permit release.

## Assessment conditions
In supported practice, discussion and AI assistance are allowed; disclose help received. The instructor provides feedback and revision opportunity.
In independent demonstration, use only the provided source/policy packet and response template. No AI assistance, external research or peer help. Access accommodations may include equivalent oral responses, accessible text and extra time; equivalent evidence is scored.
Submit reasoning and the declaration. Visual polish, writing style and speed are not separate scoring criteria.
