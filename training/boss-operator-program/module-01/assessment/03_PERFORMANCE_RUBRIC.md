# Module 01 Performance Rubric

**Version:** v1.0 candidate assessment package.
**Status:** Proposed pilot instruments; no learner outcomes or validated cut scores are claimed.

## Use
Score Stage 1, Stage 2 and the candidate-evidence explanation together.
For every score cite an observable statement or action, not confidence, fluency or terminology density.
Nine criteria use four levels: 0 absent/contradictory; 1 partial; 2 adequate independent evidence; 3 precise and integrated evidence.
Level 3 is useful diagnostic information, not required for meeting the task.

## C1 — Mission versus package (EO1)
0: Treats “fix delay handling” as the single executable update package.
1: Recognizes two goals but leaves them operationally bundled.
2: Separates the factual update from the broader improvement mission.
3: Also explains how the bounded update supports the unchanged mission without claiming to solve it.

## C2 — Package boundaries (EO2)
0: Unbounded action or no meaningful scope.
1: Names an update but leaves included/excluded actions ambiguous.
2: Specifies one-page update package, identity/version, scope and exclusions including no actual execution during assessment.
3: Also identifies change triggers requiring a new revision/package and keeps requirements traceable.

## C3 — Required inputs (EO3)
0: Uses stale S2 as current, invents source facts, or omits required authoritative input.
1: Names S1 but lacks version/provenance or necessary requirement context.
2: Uses S1 v2 as factual authority, R1/R2/P1 as governing requirements; distinguishes S2's historical role.
3: Provides precise source-to-requirement references and an explicit missing-input response.

## C4 — Destination and clarification (EO4)
0: Invents recipient/location or executes with unknown destination.
1: Notices missing destination but fails to establish receiver and location after R2.
2: Asks a relevant clarification and binds OPERATIONS_LEAD plus TRAINING_DRIVE draft folder after R2.
3: Also separates draft placement, allowed readership and final-use context without inventing wider sharing.

## C5 — Acceptance conditions (EO5)
0: Uses appearance/“looks good” as success or has no prior acceptance criteria.
1: Defines format or some content checks but misses required coverage, fidelity or delivery conditions.
2: Before generation defines page/bullet limits, all S1 facts, unknown recovery, no extra contacts/claims, source checks and location/identity evidence.
3: Makes each condition individually verifiable with an explicit evidence owner or method and unresolved-state handling.

## C6 — Over-breadth and decomposition (EO6)
0: Executes or accepts the full “fix delays” mission without bounded components.
1: Says “split it” without distinct deliverables or dependencies.
2: Isolates the update; places improvement in a separate proposed workstream needing alignment before execution.
3: Offers a bounded next improvement package with conditional inputs/destination/acceptance and preserves dependencies rather than inventing decisions.

## C7 — Scope reduction and necessary inputs (EO7)
0: Includes unnecessary contacts or silently drops required factual coverage.
1: Excludes some extra material but does not explain why the information need is preserved.
2: Excludes S3 and stale S2 facts; retains all current facts needed by the receiver; justifies each exclusion.
3: Also explains how a limited authoritative extract can satisfy the unchanged destination need without claiming new permission.

## G1 — Admission and permitted handling (candidate reframing overlay)
0: Uses an ineligible handler, guesses required permission, or proceeds while required conditions remain unresolved.
1: Mentions permission/HOLD but does not bind the decision to P1 or distinguish affected actions.
2: HOLDs unresolved update execution, refuses the prohibited external transfer, then ACCEPTs only the clarified bounded local package.
3: Precisely distinguishes package scope, entry permission, forbidden transfer and any conditional permitted alternative.

## G2 — Candidate evidence and release (candidate reframing overlay)
0: Treats generated/openable output as verified/released or invents completed evidence.
1: Recognizes a draft but omits required evidence, disposition or release authority.
2: Distinguishes format checks from source correctness, revises the unsupported Friday recovery claim, and preserves OPERATIONS_LEAD release after stated checks.
3: Explicitly ties checked/unchecked properties to candidate disposition and explains that this case's human release does not make manual approval universal.

## Provisional task decision rule
MEETS THIS TASK: all nine criteria at least 2, and no critical error.
NEEDS TARGETED REMEDIATION: assessment was validly administered, at least one criterion below 2, and no critical error.
NOT ESTABLISHED — CRITICAL ERROR: a critical error is present.
NOT ASSESSABLE: required material was not provided, independent conditions were compromised, or access needs prevented valid demonstration. Repair administration; do not record a skill failure.
No total-score average can compensate for an unresolved required criterion.
This threshold is a candidate criterion-based rule for pilot review, not a validated cut score.

## Critical errors
CE1 — Fabricated permission or proceeding with a required unresolved condition.
CE2 — Sending/proposing execution of non-PUBLIC material through the forbidden external service.
CE3 — Presenting stale/invented source content as current truth.
CE4 — Claiming verification/delivery/release evidence that has not been obtained.
CE5 — Releasing the unsupported generated recovery claim as established.
An error corrected after instructor feedback is practice progress, not evidence that the first independent attempt met criteria. Reassess on an unseen case.

## Scope of inference
A successful HOLD in Stage 1 is sound operational judgment.
MEETS THIS TASK applies to this observed sample only. It does not grant Module 01 completion, whole-program qualification or operational authorization.
Do not grade diagram polish, prose elegance or analogy use.
