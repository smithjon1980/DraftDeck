# Module 01 Pilot and Transfer Plan

**Version:** v1.0 candidate assessment package.
**Status:** Proposed pilot instruments; no learner outcomes or validated cut scores are claimed.

## Purpose
Test the usability of the exercise, clarity of the rubric and consistency of assessment. Estimate learning/transfer signals for refinement. This is a proposed plan; no participant results exist.

Suggested feasibility cohort: 6–10 consenting learners and two assessors, if available. This range is a practical starting point, not a power calculation or proof of effectiveness.

## Administration
1. Calibrate assessors with constructed specimens.
2. Establish prior experience and needed access accommodations.
3. Before instruction, administer an unseen independent case. Half receive Form A and half Form B where feasible; record assignment.
4. Teach a different worked example, then supported practice with feedback and revision.
5. After instruction, swap forms for independent performance. Do not provide the rubric key, AI help or corrective prompts during the demonstration.
6. Later, preferably 7–14 days after instruction, administer Form C with a changed context and no logistics analogy. This is a proposed interval.
7. Independently double-score the designated demonstration samples before reconciliation.
8. Record deviations, assistance and exclusions. Missing/compromised data remains explicit.
Use accessible oral/text formats with equivalent evidence. Time is descriptive, not a pass criterion.

## Form A
Use the Delay Update packet from the learner exercise, including R1 and the staged R2.
Do not use it for an unseen assessment after the learner has studied its exact key.

## Form B — Museum maintenance update

Next review is Tuesday. Completion dates NOT ESTABLISHED.
B2 — Maintenance_Status_v2, SUPERSEDED, INTERNAL:

B3 — Visitor_Contact_Export, RESTRICTED, unnecessary contacts.
Policy BP1:
INTERNAL/RESTRICTED inputs only in APPROVED_LOCAL_RUNTIME.
EXTERNAL_AI_SERVICE accepts PUBLIC inputs only and is fastest.
Receiver, location and release conditions must be established.
Minimum necessary input only; no maintenance scheduling changes.
HOLD unresolved required conditions; refuse prohibited transfers.
After initial response supply BR2:

Candidate probe: a readable six-line output states all maintenance completes Tuesday.
Expected reasoning: Tuesday is review, completion unknown; REVISE and hold release pending correction/evidence.
Score the same nine constructs with B identifiers and facts substituted. No analogy is required.

C2 — Workshop_Status_v3, SUPERSEDED, PUBLIC:

PUBLIC extracts may enter PUBLIC_APPROVED_SERVICE or APPROVED_LOCAL_RUNTIME.
RESTRICTED contacts may enter APPROVED_LOCAL_RUNTIME only and must still be necessary.
Receiver/location/content/release conditions in R1 are valid for this simulation.
No actual execution during assessment.
Correct initial admission: ACCEPT the fully bounded public-input package, excluding contacts and stale facts; no required clarification is invented.
Decomposition: broad event planning is excluded and would require separately bounded work.
Candidate probe: an eight-line output says all rooms are confirmed.
Expected reasoning: unknown allocations cannot become confirmed; format checks do not establish that claim; REVISE under the stated evidence/release conditions.

## Form C scoring adaptations
C1 separates the checklist from overall planning.
C2 bounds its identity/version, scope and exclusions.
C3 selects C1 v4 with CP1/R1.
C4 binds the already supplied destination; unnecessary clarification does not earn extra credit.
C5 states all provided content/format/fidelity/delivery conditions.
C6 identifies broad planning as separate future work rather than inventing it.
C7 excludes contacts and stale facts while preserving required coverage.
G1 ACCEPTs eligible public processing; automatic HOLD/refusal despite sufficient permission is inadequate unless a genuine unresolved condition is identified.
G2 handles the unsupported room-allocation claim as candidate error.
This altered permission is intentional: the task tests conditional reasoning, not memorized “external always forbidden.”

## Data to collect
Form/version, prior experience, administration phase, assistance, accommodations, nine criterion scores, cited evidence, critical-error categories, task disposition, descriptive duration, missing data and scorer disagreements.
Use pseudonymous learner identifiers; collect only necessary instructional data.
Do not feed instructor keys into the learner-generation notebook.

## Analysis
Report per-criterion movement and critical-error counts, with numerators/denominators.
Report learner transitions across task dispositions; keep invalid administrations separate.
Report delayed Form C performance separately.
For assessor agreement, report exact criterion agreement and pass/critical-error disagreements using first independent ratings. Preserve later reconciled decisions separately.
With small samples, emphasize cases/counts and uncertainty. Do not claim statistical validation, causality or superiority over other programs.
Before/after forms are not yet demonstrated equivalent; counterbalancing reduces an obvious order confound but does not establish equivalence.

## Refinement triggers
Any disagreement about a critical error or task disposition prompts review before using the instrument for consequential qualification decisions.
Repeated uncertainty on a criterion prompts anchor clarification and new scoring examples.
Apparent score gains without unfamiliar-context performance prompt revision of practice/transfer design.
High administration burden prompts simplification that preserves assessed constructs.
Log proposed changes, version the instruments and recalibrate after meaningful revisions.

## Completion criteria
Pilot administration complete: planned data collected or missing entries disclosed.
Pilot review complete: evidence and disagreements analyzed with changes recorded.
Instrument readiness: program authority reviews the evidence and intended-use limits.
A pilot report alone does not authorize whole-program qualification.
