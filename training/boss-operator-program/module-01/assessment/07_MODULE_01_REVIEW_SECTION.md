## Learner reading — supported teaching

### THE QUESTION
When is a request ready to become work?

A request can sound clear while leaving important decisions unstated. “Send this to our other office” names an action. It does not necessarily establish a destination. The next move depends on what the shared record already tells us and what the sender has actually authorized.

### THE CASE
Starting quickly can be reasonable. A familiar office may already have a verified address on file. A routine service may already be authorized. Repeating every question would add work without resolving anything new. The question is whether the information needed for this particular movement is established.

### WORKED CASE
The following counter scene is an illustration, not a carrier's published policy.

Shipper: “Send this box to our other office. Use the fastest service.”

Shipping clerk: “Do you mean the receiver and address on this verified record?”

Shipper: “No. That office moved. I don't have the new address yet.”

Shipping clerk: “We can prepare the declaration. We cannot dispatch it to an address we have not established. What are the contents, and are there handling requirements?”

Shipper: “Only the current documents. The older copies can stay here.”

Shipping clerk: “Then we will identify the current documents, confirm suitable packaging and an eligible service, and establish the applicable charges before dispatch. Choosing the fastest service will not supply the missing destination.”

The clerk separates preparation from dispatch. One unresolved condition stops the affected movement; it does not prevent every useful preparatory action. Speed becomes a meaningful choice after the eligible options are known.

### PRESSURE TEST
Which destination information is verified? Which record has become stale? What can be prepared while the new address remains unknown? Would paying the charges establish the correct receiver or prove delivery?

Explain each answer from the scene. A confident guess cannot replace a missing address.

### DEFINE IT
A bounded package makes one piece of information work explicit. It identifies purpose, required sources, included and excluded work, destination, handling requirements and acceptance conditions. A broad mission may need several such packages.

### THE STANDARD
REQUEST ≠ PACKAGE.
CAPABILITY ≠ PERMISSION.
GENERATED ≠ ESTABLISHED.

The counter scene maps to information work through particular relationships:

Declaration and contents → purpose, scope, required data and task. In the counter scene, current documents are distinguished from older copies. In information work, source identity and version help establish what belongs in processing.

Packaging → preparation that makes the information usable for the declared task and handler. This may include organization, format and necessary context. Good preparation does not establish that the source's claims are true.

Receiver and address → intended receiver and output location. A familiar name may be useful if a verified record supports it. An obsolete record does not establish the current destination.

Handling requirements and eligible service → data classification and permitted models, tools and environments. The agent coordinates intake and routing; the model supplies processing capability. Capability alone does not grant permission to receive the data.

Service and charges → the separate need to establish applicable resource or commercial conditions when the job requires them. Payment does not establish permission, correctness or successful delivery. An analogy does not create a universal fee requirement for information work.

Delivery evidence → evidence that the intended result reached the intended destination under the applicable acceptance and release conditions. Receipt of a physical parcel alone is not a complete model of an information result's fitness for use.

Admission governs input. Adjudication governs output. Capability governs neither by itself.

### WORKED CASE — information work
A coordinator asks for “a useful recap” of a public reading-group meeting. One reader wants the decisions; another wants the unresolved questions. The same notes can support both outputs. A fluent paragraph does not establish which need the requester meant.

An agent can use the requirements already established. If the intended coverage remains unknown, it asks a focused question. Once the package is aligned, permitted processing produces a candidate. The relevant checks then compare that candidate with the declared acceptance conditions.

### OPERATOR MOVE
Read the request for the need. Examine the record for what is established. Mark the required decisions that remain unresolved. Describe the next action supported by that record, including its scope and permitted handling. After generation, distinguish what has been checked from what still needs evidence.

Apply that reasoning to the information-work task below. You do not need to perform a shipping role or repeat the counter vocabulary.

### BOUNDARY
The scene makes a control relationship visible. Actual data handling follows the applicable information policies. A shipping payment is not AI authorization; a physical address is not a complete acceptance specification; a delivery notification is not evidence that every factual claim in a generated result is correct.

Neither every missing detail nor every external service requires HOLD. Required unresolved conditions and the actual permission policy govern the affected action. Routine choices may proceed within an established authorization envelope.

### Setting
All people, files, quantities and policies below are synthetic training fixtures. This is a planning simulation. Do not upload source material to another service, generate the final update, send messages or change operational records.

You receive a request through an AI-work interface. Act as the person governing the request and the admission agent's decisions. Use ordinary information-work language; no logistics analogy is needed.

### Initial request — R1
“Make these files into a one-page update and send it wherever it would be useful. Use whatever AI is fastest. While you are at it, fix how we handle delays.”

### Source packet
S1 — Delay_Status_v2, CURRENT, INTERNAL.
Authoritative current facts:
- Four orders are delayed.
- Three await supplier confirmation; one awaits an internal scheduling decision.
- The next status review is Friday.
- Recovery dates are NOT ESTABLISHED.
- Scope: facts for the current update only; no authority to change operations.

S2 — Delay_Status_v1, SUPERSEDED, INTERNAL.
Earlier facts: six delayed orders; estimated recovery Thursday.
The version header explicitly says superseded by S1. These facts are not current.

S3 — Contact_Export, RESTRICTED, unrelated to update facts.
Contains synthetic personal contact records. No contact details are needed to explain the current delay status.

### Handling policy — P1
- INTERNAL and RESTRICTED material may be processed only within APPROVED_LOCAL_RUNTIME.
- EXTERNAL_AI_SERVICE may process PUBLIC material only. Nothing in this packet is classified PUBLIC.
- The fastest service is EXTERNAL_AI_SERVICE. Speed does not change permission.
- The receiver and output location must be established by the requester. Do not invent them.
- Sharing beyond the declared receiver requires a separate authorization.
- Only necessary data may enter processing.
- Required unresolved admission conditions produce HOLD for the affected action.
- A proposed forbidden transfer is REFUSED; a permitted alternative may be packaged separately.
- Final release authority and intended-use acceptance conditions must be established before the generated candidate is used.

### Stage 1 submission
Before any execution, submit:
1. Known requirements with source IDs.
2. Required unknowns and why each matters.
3. A bounded package draft separating the update from the broader improvement mission.
4. Initial admission disposition and its scope: which action is held or refused?
5. Up to four targeted questions that would resolve required conditions. If more are essential, justify the additions.

Then stop. The instructor supplies a standardized clarification. The number of questions is a focus target, not a penalty for necessary clarification.

### Candidate evidence question
Imagine a later generated update is one page and opens correctly. It states “all four orders will recover Friday.”
Without executing anything, explain:
- what the page/open checks establish;
- what the source establishes about recovery;
- the candidate disposition and corrective evidence needed;
- why successful generation does not itself permit release.

### Standardized clarification — R2
Provide after Stage 1 to every learner:
“The update is for OPERATIONS_LEAD in TRAINING_DRIVE, draft folder. Use the current facts in S1. Make a one-page update of no more than five bullets covering the delayed count, cause breakdown, next review and unknown recovery dates. Use APPROVED_LOCAL_RUNTIME only. Do not include contact details and do not change scheduling. Prepare only the update package now; put delay-process improvement in a separate proposed workstream. OPERATIONS_LEAD authorizes final release after source-faithfulness and coverage checks. Until then, any generated update is a draft candidate.”

This is a synthetic role, not a verified real recipient. It grants no actual external-service permission.

### Stage 1 acceptable reasoning
Known: one-page update requested; bundled improvement mission; S1 current/internal; S2 superseded; S3 restricted/unnecessary; fastest external service is ineligible.
Unknown: receiver, location, intended coverage/acceptance, release conditions.
HOLD the update execution while required conditions are unresolved.
REFUSE the forbidden transfer to EXTERNAL_AI_SERVICE under P1.
A package draft is permitted; source transmission and final production are not.
Do not require a specific phrasing if action scope and rationale are correct.

Useful questions establish destination, output purpose/coverage, boundaries of the improvement request and review/release authority. Several requirements may be resolved in one clear question.

### Stage 2 adequate package
Locally assigned package identity/version.
Purpose: factual current-delay update for OPERATIONS_LEAD.
Scope: package for one-page, at-most-five-bullet update only.
Inputs: S1 v2 with R1, R2 and P1 as governing requirements. S2 is provenance evidence for exclusion, not factual input to the update. S3 is unnecessary and excluded.
Handling: INTERNAL, APPROVED_LOCAL_RUNTIME only.
Destination: OPERATIONS_LEAD; TRAINING_DRIVE draft folder.
Admission: ACCEPT for the bounded clarified package under the simulation. This is entry permission, not final-output release.
Excluded: contact data, stale facts, external processing, scheduling changes, broad process redesign, wider sharing and actual execution during assessment.
Acceptance:
- one page, at most five bullets;
- count four; breakdown three supplier/one scheduling;
- Friday identified as next review, not recovery;
- recovery dates explicitly NOT ESTABLISHED;
- no invented dates, contacts or unsupported causal explanation;
- required coverage checked against S1 and R2;
- output identity/version and target location checked;
- OPERATIONS_LEAD release after required evidence.
Evidence is planned, not fabricated.

### Decomposition
The learner should flag “fix delay handling” as too broad for the update package.
A bounded first improvement package could inventory current delay-handling steps, but required inputs, scope, destination and acceptance must be aligned before execution. Do not score an invented improvement plan as established.
The destination's factual information need stays intact when unnecessary input material is removed.

### Candidate vignette key
One-page/open checks establish format/accessibility properties only.
S1 establishes next review Friday and unknown recovery dates.
“All four recover Friday” is unsupported. REVISE the candidate; hold its release pending correction and source-faithfulness verification. REJECT with a reasoned correction route is also defensible.
No universal human-review requirement is inferred; this particular fixture explicitly assigns OPERATIONS_LEAD release authority.

### Remediation
C1/C2/C6: distinguish mission, bounded update and separate workstream.
C3/C7: annotate current/stale/unnecessary sources.
C4: practice targeted clarification.
C5: rewrite vague “looks good” criteria as observable checks.
G1: match classification to eligible environments.
G2: distinguish generation, evidence, candidate disposition and release.

After practice, use unseen alternate material for an independent reassessment. Record the assistance level and form; do not reuse a memorized key as novel evidence.

## Instructor performance rubric — assessment criteria
### Use
Score Stage 1, Stage 2 and the candidate-evidence explanation together.
For every score cite an observable statement or action, not confidence, fluency or terminology density.
Nine criteria use four levels: 0 absent/contradictory; 1 partial; 2 adequate independent evidence; 3 precise and integrated evidence.
Level 3 is useful diagnostic information, not required for meeting the task.

### C1 — Mission versus package (EO1)
0: Treats “fix delay handling” as the single executable update package.
1: Recognizes two goals but leaves them operationally bundled.
2: Separates the factual update from the broader improvement mission.
3: Also explains how the bounded update supports the unchanged mission without claiming to solve it.

### C2 — Package boundaries (EO2)
0: Unbounded action or no meaningful scope.
1: Names an update but leaves included/excluded actions ambiguous.
2: Specifies one-page update package, identity/version, scope and exclusions including no actual execution during assessment.
3: Also identifies change triggers requiring a new revision/package and keeps requirements traceable.

### C3 — Required inputs (EO3)
0: Uses stale S2 as current, invents source facts, or omits required authoritative input.
1: Names S1 but lacks version/provenance or necessary requirement context.
2: Uses S1 v2 as factual authority, R1/R2/P1 as governing requirements; distinguishes S2's historical role.
3: Provides precise source-to-requirement references and an explicit missing-input response.

### C4 — Destination and clarification (EO4)
0: Invents recipient/location or executes with unknown destination.
1: Notices missing destination but fails to establish receiver and location after R2.
2: Asks a relevant clarification and binds OPERATIONS_LEAD plus TRAINING_DRIVE draft folder after R2.
3: Also separates draft placement, allowed readership and final-use context without inventing wider sharing.

### C5 — Acceptance conditions (EO5)
0: Uses appearance/“looks good” as success or has no prior acceptance criteria.
1: Defines format or some content checks but misses required coverage, fidelity or delivery conditions.
2: Before generation defines page/bullet limits, all S1 facts, unknown recovery, no extra contacts/claims, source checks and location/identity evidence.
3: Makes each condition individually verifiable with an explicit evidence owner or method and unresolved-state handling.

### C6 — Over-breadth and decomposition (EO6)
0: Executes or accepts the full “fix delays” mission without bounded components.
1: Says “split it” without distinct deliverables or dependencies.
2: Isolates the update; places improvement in a separate proposed workstream needing alignment before execution.
3: Offers a bounded next improvement package with conditional inputs/destination/acceptance and preserves dependencies rather than inventing decisions.

### C7 — Scope reduction and necessary inputs (EO7)
0: Includes unnecessary contacts or silently drops required factual coverage.
1: Excludes some extra material but does not explain why the information need is preserved.
2: Excludes S3 and stale S2 facts; retains all current facts needed by the receiver; justifies each exclusion.
3: Also explains how a limited authoritative extract can satisfy the unchanged destination need without claiming new permission.

### G1 — Admission and permitted handling (candidate reframing overlay)
0: Uses an ineligible handler, guesses required permission, or proceeds while required conditions remain unresolved.
1: Mentions permission/HOLD but does not bind the decision to P1 or distinguish affected actions.
2: HOLDs unresolved update execution, refuses the prohibited external transfer, then ACCEPTs only the clarified bounded local package.
3: Precisely distinguishes package scope, entry permission, forbidden transfer and any conditional permitted alternative.

### G2 — Candidate evidence and release (candidate reframing overlay)
0: Treats generated/openable output as verified/released or invents completed evidence.
1: Recognizes a draft but omits required evidence, disposition or release authority.
2: Distinguishes format checks from source correctness, revises the unsupported Friday recovery claim, and preserves OPERATIONS_LEAD release after stated checks.
3: Explicitly ties checked/unchecked properties to candidate disposition and explains that this case's human release does not make manual approval universal.

### Provisional task decision rule
MEETS THIS TASK: all nine criteria at least 2, and no critical error.
NEEDS TARGETED REMEDIATION: assessment was validly administered, at least one criterion below 2, and no critical error.
NOT ESTABLISHED — CRITICAL ERROR: a critical error is present.
NOT ASSESSABLE: required material was not provided, independent conditions were compromised, or access needs prevented valid demonstration. Repair administration; do not record a skill failure.
No total-score average can compensate for an unresolved required criterion.
This threshold is a candidate criterion-based rule for pilot review, not a validated cut score.

### Critical errors
CE1 — Fabricated permission or proceeding with a required unresolved condition.
CE2 — Sending/proposing execution of non-PUBLIC material through the forbidden external service.
CE3 — Presenting stale/invented source content as current truth.
CE4 — Claiming verification/delivery/release evidence that has not been obtained.
CE5 — Releasing the unsupported generated recovery claim as established.
An error corrected after instructor feedback is practice progress, not evidence that the first independent attempt met criteria. Reassess on an unseen case.

### Scope of inference
A successful HOLD in Stage 1 is sound operational judgment.
MEETS THIS TASK applies to this observed sample only. It does not grant Module 01 completion, whole-program qualification or operational authorization.
Do not grade diagram polish, prose elegance or analogy use.
