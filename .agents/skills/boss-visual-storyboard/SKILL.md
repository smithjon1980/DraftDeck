# BOSS Visual Storyboard — illustrated narrative director

## Authority
Read `AGENTS.md`, `design-system/boss-design-contract.json`, `.agents/skills/boss-story-architect/SKILL.md`, and `skills/draftdeck/references/editorial-composition.md`. The selected creative language is **white-ground, art-led technical editorial illustration**, informed by Tesla Model Y center-screen information architecture and CAD/ISO drafting. The user explicitly selected the **new cyan/magenta/black BOSS wordmark** for the current Slide 02 exploration. This selection does not prove a production-ready source lockup or retire the original Bioscillate logo; track both identities separately.

## Mandatory image-medium decision BEFORE generation
Classify the proposed medium explicitly:
- **EDITORIAL_ILLUSTRATION (default and required for Episode 01 narrative scenes):** deliberately drawn scenes with visible construction, sectional cutaways, process consequences, material form, humane but stylized figures as needed, fine linework and sparse brand-color accents.
- **TECHNICAL_DIAGRAM:** supporting explanatory mechanism with measured geometry and labels; not a substitute for the whole illustrated story.
- **CONCEPT_COMP:** temporary complete image for visual selection; not a production slide, may contain provisional typography.
- **PHOTOGRAPHY/PHOTOREALISM:** rejected for contemporary BOSS Slide 02 unless the human explicitly changes the medium. Avoid stock photography, photo-collages, cinematic actors, 3D glossy renders, luminous data center dashboards, dark cyberpunk HUDs, gradients, and generic SaaS cards.

A medium choice is a first-class **creative gate**; provenance and image presence alone cannot override a medium mismatch. Do not put the wrong image on a white page and call it repaired.

## Build the illustrated story
1. Read Bridge 1: identify a concrete cause/action/evidence/outcome relationship that the learner can understand at a glance. Show *why* capability delivers value rather than drawing two groups of professionals.
2. For Slide 02, propose a **single coherent cross-sectional/axonometric Market Seven convenience-retail scene**: on one side relationships/opportunity are potential; on the other a particular operational business problem moves through **LEARN → BUILD → VERIFY → DEPLOY → SUPPORT**, with an inspectable proof artifact and an operating result. Avoid invented numeric improvements; show only conceptual checks and outcomes, not factual KPI claims.
3. Make the work visually substantial. Detail shelves/POS counter, an operator's real task, service implementation, a documented test, and a functioning system. Use no visual clichés such as generic speech-bubble networks substituting for a store.
4. White background (#FFFFFF), black/pencil-ink drafting geometry, fine CAD hairlines, reserved whitespace and small cyan/magenta emphasis only. No colored scene backdrops or decorative gradients. Treat the Tesla inspiration as hierarchy and zoning—not a copy of Tesla UI branding.
5. Describe protected crop zones and intended legibility at 1920×1080. A compelling illustration must survive with **all text hidden**.
6. Concept composition may suggest layout, but production art must be separate from editorial headline, paragraphs, reflection and both brand lockups. Reserve live HTML text zones. Preserve user-selected BOSS wordmark design as a separate named asset; do not redraw it silently or conflate it with the original Bioscillate mark.
7. Prepare one candidate unless a four-way comparison is requested. Report narrative choices, source claims versus proposals, and deviations before proposing approval.

## Fail-closed creative inspection
Reject any reference that:
- uses photography or photorealistic people without explicit medium change;
- substitutes generic comparison cards/icons or diagrams for substantial story art;
- changes brand ground to dark or introduces neon/gradient-filled scenes;
- depicts no specific *working, tested, delivered* capability;
- reproduces a whole generated slide as final artwork with baked editorial text;
- fabricates approval, metrics, verified citations, label meanings or identity equivalence.

Deliver a concise storyboard: `medium`, `teaching_relationship`, `operational_scene`, `visible_evidence_of_working_capability`, `style_reference`, `anti_style`, `crop_safe_regions`, `text_reserved_regions`, `identity_assets`, and `source_status`. Handoff to `boss-art-asset-producer`. Passing the medium gate means *eligible for inspection*, never approved.
