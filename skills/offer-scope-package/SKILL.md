# Offer Scope and Delivery Package

## Goal
Compose a differentiated deliverable from individually justified solutions.

## Proof
Every included service, deliverable and bonus has owner, effort/cost, acceptance criterion and clear exclusion.

## Steps
1. Choose solution components.
2. prune weak or expensive additions.
3. bundle coherent outputs.
4. document prerequisites and terms.
5. evaluate customer effort..

## Boundary
No fabricated value stacking, unbounded service obligation or hidden dependencies.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: documented business context, source-bound discovery notes, and authorized scope.
Output: an independently authored candidate workpaper with source references, assumptions and unresolved items.

pages 71–77; HORMOZI-100M-OFFERS-2021-UK-001. Source content is an author's commercial methodology, **not** independently verified BOSS evidence. The operational procedure here is an original BOSS adaptation, not reproduced book text.
