# Continuity Promise Liability Review

## Goal
Evaluate recurring service, fixed-price and lifetime terms as long-lived delivery liabilities.

## Proof
Every continuity promise has limits, cost sensitivity, termination/renewal rules and escalation owner; irreversible open-ended obligations rejected.

## Steps
1. Describe recurring value.
2. record price and direct cost ranges.
3. simulate changing demand and costs.
4. assess service continuity under cancellations.
5. document transparent remedies and bounded terms.

## Boundary
Do not recommend lifetime promises without sufficient pricing, authority and cost evidence.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: authorized and minimized first-party business data or synthetic lab records, declared source provenance, and permissible scope.
Output: a self-contained BOSS research workpaper with assumptions, metrics, declared uncertainty and a qualified recommendation or HOLD.

## Falsification
Any required economic, authorization or independent evidence operand absent → HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED. Any asserted settled financial outcome without independently reconcilable records → G5_HOLD.
