# First Free-to-Paid Proof

## Goal
Convert an exploratory free trial into an original minimal paid offer only after real user feedback.

## Proof
Distinct records for trial, satisfaction, priced offer, consent, completed sale and settled payment; synthetic examples not represented as buyers.

## Steps
1. Offer a bounded prototype.
2. observe trial behavior.
3. record needs.
4. price a V1 with sustainable delivery cost.
5. request purchase independently.
6. reconcile any actual payment.

## Boundary
No pressure to charge before product fit; no free-trial feedback counted as actual paid demand or verified cash yield.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: relevant, authorized small-business observations or synthetic lab records.
Output: a bounded, source-linked candidate workpaper with evidence status and HOLD conditions.

## Falsification
If the workpaper advances a stage or promotes execution without the stated proof, or converts illustrative estimates into verified sales, income or success, return `G5_HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
