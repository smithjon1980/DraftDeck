## Fail-closed decisions
- Photograph/dark-HUD artwork after a no-photo brief → **FAIL: MEDIUM** before typography/export testing.
- Missing teaching relationship or art → **FAIL: STORY**.
- Missing real separate art/incorrect original logo/unverified bytes → **HOLD: ASSET**.
- Wrong or rasterized required copy / placeholder reflection → **FAIL: EDITORIAL**.
- Cropping, overflow or mismatch to selected concept → **FAIL: COMPOSITION**.
- No approved visual master / no edit-save-reopen adapter proof / no explicit human release authority → **HOLD**.
