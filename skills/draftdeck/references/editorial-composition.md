# Editorial composition — source to story to BOSS

**Status:** skill guidance candidate, not approval of any slide or manuscript.

## Admission record
Capture source name/version, checksum when available, page/paragraph, evidence status (verified / candidate / unavailable), exact-copy boundaries, source art identity and approval state. Distinguish original audio/transcript, OCR/captions, editorial paraphrase and NotebookLM spark. Unknown remains UNKNOWN. Do not silently exchange candidate prose for recorded words.

## Two bridges
**Bridge 1 (meaning):** extract the teachable claim, constraint, observation, uncertainty and learner action. Reimagine a grounded business scenario, using Market Seven when useful, without fabricating facts. Identify which mechanical/business relationship the artwork must communicate.

**Bridge 2 (composition):** map the story to a BOSS visual reading hierarchy. Choose art-led, narrative-led or hybrid depending on the story. Preserve important relationships, including diagrams and sequential mechanisms, without cropping them for graphic impact.

## Layer plan and semantic structure
Separate (a) source art, (b) teaching story and annotations, (c) CAD drafting grid/title plate, (d) brand masthead, (e) editable primary copy, (f) evidence notes, and (g) Pause and Consider / learner notes. Live text must retain meaningful headings, paragraphs and reading order. Semantic HTML aids accessibility and inspection but does not by itself establish visual quality.

## Rejection tests
Reject a candidate that removes a teaching relationship, replaces rich approved artwork with box/line shorthand, omits approved identity, invents sources, alters exact scripted copy, or hides reflection prompts. Examine the *actual render*, not solely JSX or PDF extractable text. A beautiful diagram can still be a failed teaching diagram.

## Learning loop
Each teaching unit should, when appropriate, provide a short explanation, worked example, learner challenge, observable check and reflection. The instructor's HTML/CSS video supplied the exercise/checkpoint pattern; it is pedagogy inspiration, not a BOSS implementation authority.
