# Offer Sequence Eligibility

## Goal
Determine whether a follow-on service addresses a newly documented customer need and is independently fulfillable.

## Proof
Every additional service has distinct customer consent, measurable need, disclosed cost, independent fulfillment gate and stop decision.

## Steps
1. Establish first delivered outcome.
2. document new need.
3. compare no-purchase and simpler options.
4. check capacity and pricing.
5. request explicit consent.
6. hold if need unproved.

## Boundary
No forced upsell, bait-and-switch or dependency designed to prevent cancellation.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: authorized and minimized first-party business data or synthetic lab records, declared source provenance, and permissible scope.
Output: a self-contained BOSS research workpaper with assumptions, metrics, declared uncertainty and a qualified recommendation or HOLD.

## Falsification
Any required economic, authorization or independent evidence operand absent → HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED. Any asserted settled financial outcome without independently reconcilable records → G5_HOLD.
