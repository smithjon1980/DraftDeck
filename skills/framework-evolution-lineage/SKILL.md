# Framework Evolution Lineage

## Goal
Trace changes among multiple sketches without mistaking abandoned drafts for canonical doctrine.

## Proof
Given successive sketches, produce versioned concepts, retained/removed distinctions, uncertainty and one explicitly selected candidate; never auto-ratify.

## Steps
1. Locate successive diagrams and their headings.
2. identify stable concepts vs experiments.
3. record conflicts and image/page references.
4. rebuild one coherent candidate.
5. attach decision/authorization status.

## Boundary
A visually later page is not proof of later creation or final authority. No automatic promotion of draft sketches.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: inspected journal pages and source-linked business context.
Output: an original, bounded, independently reviewable candidate artifact.

## Source provenance
HORMOZI-100M-JOURNAL-2023-001; journal pp. 8–24 (evolving tables of contents) and pp. 54–78 (lead-framework restructuring). The journal is exploratory and its drawings are not independently validated operational procedures.

## Falsification
A proposed capability with ungrounded labels, missing denominators, unsupported profit claims or omitted authority boundaries is rejected/HOLD instead of promoted.
