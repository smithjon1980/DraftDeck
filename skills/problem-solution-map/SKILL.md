# Problem-to-Solution Mapping

## Goal
Convert an observed buyer journey into a complete, prioritized problem-to-intervention map.

## Proof
Every proposed intervention has a recorded customer problem, step, constraint and acceptance test.

## Steps
1. Map before/during/after customer journey.
2. enumerate concrete problems.
3. form alternative solutions including no automation.
4. estimate feasibility and cost.
5. choose bounded candidate..

## Boundary
Do not manufacture customer complaints or force automation; missing evidence is NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: documented business context, source-bound discovery notes, and authorized scope.
Output: an independently authored candidate workpaper with source references, assumptions and unresolved items.
