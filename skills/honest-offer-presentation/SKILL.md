# Honest Offer Presentation

## Goal
Translate a verified scope into an understandable buyer-facing offer.

## Proof
Claims trace to documented deliverables; urgency/bonuses/name do not distort terms; claims survive adverse-example review.

## Steps
1. Draft outcome-first language.
2. check limits and prerequisites.
3. test comprehension.
4. document genuine capacity/deadline facts.
5. version offer copy.
6. review misleading examples.

## Prerequisite — fulfillment authorization
A commercial offer cannot be proposed for authorized public routing unless `offer-fulfillment-tradeoff` has a current, traceable Automation Suitability Gate and Phase 4 VALUE result. `UNPROFITABLE_FULFILLMENT_TRIP` or `HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED` blocks public presentation/deployment. Internal exploratory wording may still be drafted, prominently marked UNAPPROVED / RESEARCH_ONLY. This is a candidate documentation requirement; there is no implemented runtime broker enforcement.

## G5 Evidence Integrity Gate — claim assembly
Before any external-facing copy is eligible for approval, classify each factual claim as `VERIFIED_OUTCOME`, `SUPPORTED_CAPABILITY`, `QUALIFIED_PROJECTION`, or `UNVERIFIED_PROJECTION`. For *past performance / realized outcome* claims, require a resolvable digest-linked independently reviewed outcome receipt, with lineage to admitted governing source/policy, declared measurement method, applicable cohort, denominator and period. **A source document's L0 hash does not itself prove a business outcome**, and a Lens B probe pass does not prove general market performance.

`UNVERIFIED_PROJECTION` is prohibited from being presented as established performance or as an unconditional promise. Hypothetical projections may appear only as explicitly labeled scenarios with documented assumptions, uncertainty, no guaranteed-results language and an authorized review; otherwise `G5_HOLD`. If the source-to-receipt chain cannot be resolved, do not silently fill it. No marketing claims are auto-published by this skill.

**Falsification:** A promotional number with only the Hormozi book source hash, or an experimental booking estimate, is output as verified BOSS revenue → fail closed.

## Boundary
No fabricated scarcity or urgency; promotional anecdotes are unverified and never treated as BOSS results.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: documented business context, source-bound discovery notes, and authorized scope.
Output: an independently authored candidate workpaper with source references, assumptions and unresolved items.
