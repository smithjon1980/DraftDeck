# Single Channel Demand Stabilization

## Goal
Test one understandable acquisition method for a tiny business before adding multiple channels or a complex stack.

## Proof
Defined channel, campaign window, lawful reach, conversion denominator, customer cost and pause threshold.

## Steps
1. Choose accessible channel.
2. create clear honest message.
3. run consent-compliant small experiment.
4. log actual inquiries and conversions.
5. compare cost and quality.
6. stop or iterate.

## Boundary
No fixed numerical marketing ritual required; no assumed causal attribution or spam.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: relevant, authorized small-business observations or synthetic lab records.
Output: a bounded, source-linked candidate workpaper with evidence status and HOLD conditions.

## Falsification
If the workpaper advances a stage or promotes execution without the stated proof, or converts illustrative estimates into verified sales, income or success, return `G5_HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
