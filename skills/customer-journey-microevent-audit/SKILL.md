# Customer Journey Microevent Audit

## Goal
Turn observed customer steps and delays into measured friction points.

## Proof
An observed before/after comparison with comparable conditions and explicit total elapsed time; no claimed savings from estimates alone.

## Steps
1. Interview operator/customer with permission.
2. enumerate preceding and following tasks.
3. timestamp test events.
4. identify wait and effort.
5. propose one simpler flow.
6. verify independently against baseline.

## Boundary
Do not infer completed worksheet answers from unanswered prompts; no market success, consent, operating ability, authentic source admission or financial receipt may be invented. No broker execution or outside publication from this candidate. Missing required input → HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## Human–agent correspondence
Human dependency: Human supplies a real journey, timestamps and what felt burdensome.
Agent deliverable: Agent delivers timestamped step/baseline/alternative matrix, separating measured versus estimated effects.
Correction: Authenticated human correction of step, timing or assumptions; persist override in proposed event contract, invalidate stale derivation and HOLD for review.
Human approval is required before any external/financial operation. Proposed SEAM-B01 cache invalidation and SOCIAL controls are not runtime implemented.
