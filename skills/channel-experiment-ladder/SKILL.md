# Channel Experiment Ladder

## Goal
Choose and test the smallest appropriate outreach channel before scaling.

## Proof
One defined segment/channel/message, baseline and stop rule exist; additions require observed results rather than unverified audience assumptions.

## Steps
1. Select segment and one channel.
2. choose manual low-cost experiment.
3. record outreach volume and permissions.
4. compare results and unit costs.
5. modify or stop.
6. only then consider adding another channel.

## Boundary
No spam, artificial pressure, intrusive cold outreach or inferred market dominance. Results may remain inconclusive.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: inspected journal pages and source-linked business context.
Output: an original, bounded, independently reviewable candidate artifact.
