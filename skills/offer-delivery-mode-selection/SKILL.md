# Offer Delivery Mode Selection

## Goal
Choose human-delivered, assisted or reusable delivery vehicle based on service quality and fulfillment economics.

## Proof
At least one deliverable option has mapped problem, real owner, costs, quality criteria and rollback.

## Steps
1. List original service alternatives.
2. map each to a real problem.
3. estimate time and cost-to-serve.
4. compare alternatives including manual.
5. recommend bounded delivery trial.
6. request approval.
