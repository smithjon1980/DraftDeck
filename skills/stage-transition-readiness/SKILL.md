# Stage Transition Readiness

## Goal
Define measurable entry and exit checks before expanding business operating complexity.

## Proof
Each transition names existing constraint, acceptance event, operator, negative test and rollback/HOLD.

## Steps
1. Identify current limitation.
2. define an observable stage-graduation test.
3. collect baseline.
4. perform a small reversible improvement.
5. inspect results.
6. update stage record.

## Boundary
Never force a business through all ten stages; a successful event is not proof of sustainable economic fitness.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: relevant, authorized small-business observations or synthetic lab records.
Output: a bounded, source-linked candidate workpaper with evidence status and HOLD conditions.

## Falsification
If the workpaper advances a stage or promotes execution without the stated proof, or converts illustrative estimates into verified sales, income or success, return `G5_HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
