# Owner-Operator Role Boundary

## Goal
Clarify responsibilities between producing work, supervising it and verifying results in a small office.

## Proof
Each role has named decision scope, accountable handoff, observable metric and safe escalation without requiring extra staff.

## Steps
1. List maker/operator and manager/reviewer tasks.
2. define minimal separation appropriate to one- or two-person shop.
3. identify overburden and task interruptions.
4. document training and escalation.
5. periodically review constraints.

## Boundary
Do not invent enterprise personnel; an owner may hold multiple roles but must record sensitive approval separately.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: authorized and minimized first-party business data or synthetic lab records, declared source provenance, and permissible scope.
Output: a self-contained BOSS research workpaper with assumptions, metrics, declared uncertainty and a qualified recommendation or HOLD.

## Falsification
Any required economic, authorization or independent evidence operand absent → HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED. Any asserted settled financial outcome without independently reconcilable records → G5_HOLD.
