# Bottleneck Priority Review

## Goal
Find the single most consequential business constraint and evaluate the smallest next intervention.

## Proof
Backlog has one selected constraint, evidence-backed ranking, cost-to-serve estimate, deferred ideas and falsification condition.

## Steps
1. Survey problems.
2. estimate impact and controllability.
3. compare manual process improvement and tool options.
4. choose one bounded intervention.
5. retain rejected alternatives with rationale.

## Boundary
No automatic scale-up or feature additions merely because a later roadmap stage exists.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: relevant, authorized small-business observations or synthetic lab records.
Output: a bounded, source-linked candidate workpaper with evidence status and HOLD conditions.

## Falsification
If the workpaper advances a stage or promotes execution without the stated proof, or converts illustrative estimates into verified sales, income or success, return `G5_HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
