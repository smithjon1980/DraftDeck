# Real Capacity and Promotion Check

## Goal
Stop fabricated scarcity, deadline pressure and unsupported bonus valuations in small business offers.

## Proof
Scarcity, deadline and partner-bonus claims are backed by contemporaneous capacity/permission records or excluded.

## Steps
1. Identify offered constraint.
2. verify inventory or appointment capacity.
3. review time-bound facts and partner consent.
4. inspect claimed bonus values.
5. calculate deliverability.
6. approve or HOLD.

## Boundary
Do not infer completed worksheet answers from unanswered prompts; no market success, consent, operating ability, authentic source admission or financial receipt may be invented. No broker execution or outside publication from this candidate. Missing required input → HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## Human–agent correspondence
Human dependency: Human supplies genuine capacity limits, offer duration, partner permission and contractual bounds.
Agent deliverable: Agent presents capacity and offer-terms verification sheet with missing evidence and rejection findings.
Correction: Human may correct real capacities and terms through authenticated review, not retroactively fabricate them.
Human approval is required before any external/financial operation. Proposed SEAM-B01 cache invalidation and SOCIAL controls are not runtime implemented.
