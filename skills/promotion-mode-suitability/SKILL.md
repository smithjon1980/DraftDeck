# Promotion Mode Suitability

## Goal
Select between premium, free, and discounted trial offers with a real cost and expectation baseline.

## Proof
For each candidate promotion record delivery cost, acquisition cost, eligibility, offer terms, conversion denominator and stop threshold.

## Steps
1. Define promotional goal.
2. document simple free/premium/discount alternatives.
3. estimate cost of fulfillment and downstream support.
4. run an authorized synthetic comparison.
5. recommend trial only if costs and promises are bounded.

## Boundary
Reject fictitious urgency, deceptive savings or promotion that cannot be fulfilled.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: authorized and minimized first-party business data or synthetic lab records, declared source provenance, and permissible scope.
Output: a self-contained BOSS research workpaper with assumptions, metrics, declared uncertainty and a qualified recommendation or HOLD.

## Falsification
Any required economic, authorization or independent evidence operand absent → HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED. Any asserted settled financial outcome without independently reconcilable records → G5_HOLD.
