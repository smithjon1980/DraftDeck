# Offer Workbook Validation

## Goal
Convert a completed commercial worksheet into a traceable, falsifiable business design workpaper.

## Proof
Every adopted worksheet assumption has source question, human answer or UNANSWERED status, evaluation criterion and distinct fact/inference flag.

## Steps
1. Admit source identity without claiming authenticated L0.
2. enumerate exercises.
3. distinguish questions from correct answers.
4. associate each answer with candidate skill.
5. flag unsupported conclusions.
6. request human signoff.

## Boundary
Do not infer completed worksheet answers from unanswered prompts; no market success, consent, operating ability, authentic source admission or financial receipt may be invented. No broker execution or outside publication from this candidate. Missing required input → HOLD / NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## Human–agent correspondence
Human dependency: Human supplies genuine answers and source/rights, correcting any misunderstood buyer need.
Agent deliverable: Agent outputs a source-anchored question-answer, evidence/uncertainty and approval matrix; no invented filled answers.
Correction: Human rejects wrong row via authenticated correction; new derivative keeps original answer history and requires reinspection.
Human approval is required before any external/financial operation. Proposed SEAM-B01 cache invalidation and SOCIAL controls are not runtime implemented.
