# Owner-to-Trainer Handoff

## Goal
Turn a founder's repeatable manual workflow into a teachable operator checklist and acceptance test.

## Proof
A second authorized operator can complete a test task with documented exceptions and measured error rates.

## Steps
1. Observe owner workflow.
2. list decisions and exceptions.
3. produce brief SOP.
4. teach with synthetic tasks.
5. observe handoff.
6. revise instructions.

## Boundary
No enterprise staffing assumption or uncontrolled delegation; real customer privileges require authorization.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: relevant, authorized small-business observations or synthetic lab records.
Output: a bounded, source-linked candidate workpaper with evidence status and HOLD conditions.
