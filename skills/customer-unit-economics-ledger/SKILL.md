# Customer Unit Economics Ledger

## Goal
Keep acquisition cost, lifetime gross profit and payback separate and traceable.

## Proof
For a defined cohort, store acquisition spend, attributable acquired customers, recognized revenue, direct costs, observation window and reconciliation status.

## Steps
1. Specify cohort and inclusion.
2. record cash-flow timeline.
3. separate realized from estimated data.
4. calculate CAC, LTGP and payback only with compatible denominators.
5. report uncertainty and alternatives.

## Boundary
No attribution of recovered employee hours as realized cash; no inferred future profit presented as settled financial yield.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: authorized and minimized first-party business data or synthetic lab records, declared source provenance, and permissible scope.
Output: a self-contained BOSS research workpaper with assumptions, metrics, declared uncertainty and a qualified recommendation or HOLD.

## Falsification
