# BOSS Day Zero Audio — Co-Developed Competency Rubric v0.1

**Status:** WORKING / HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING · October 13, 2026  
**Scope:** Shared educational destination for DZ-AUDIO-01 (*The Discipline of Constraint*) and DZ-AUDIO-02 (*The Human-Centered AI Triad*).  
**Pedagogical method:** **Know the end at the beginning, then build backward while checking forward.**

## Begin with the end

**Proposed terminal learner performance:** The learner can take a serious, bounded project question, intentionally define constraints and role authority, use one or more AI models for proposals and critique, reconcile their output with actual source evidence, explain and authorize a decision, conduct or specify a falsifiable test, and archive a transferable decision/evidence record without claiming unobserved success.

That is the **destination**, not a claim that listeners achieve it by hearing the podcast.

## The co-development rule

**Rubric development is part of audio development**, not a separate prerequisite that must be finished beforehand:

1. **Define the intended end capability first.** The human reviews or revises this statement before editorial script lock.
2. **Propose measurable competencies**, then map them to episode segments, examples and external learner actions.
3. **Draft the episode and its assessment together.** If a segment cannot support a competency, revise either its lesson design or the proposed rubric with a change log and rationale.
4. **Use NotebookLM/Gemini as private creative sparks and critics.** Let them identify missing connections, but they cannot declare rubric validity or authorize the final script.
5. **Add formative checks** while scripting: conceptual questions, evidence discrimination and reflection.
6. **Construct an actual learner work product** after/beside the listening experience; do not infer mastery from completion or audio duration alone.
7. **Review outcome coverage before script lock**, then human approves the current rubric version for that episode.
8. **Improve the rubric from observed trials** while preserving baseline criteria, versioned evidence, and the reasons for any standard changes. Do not silently lower thresholds to declare success.

This is disciplined iterative construction within a predefined educational destination, not unconstrained improvisation and not a requirement for a pre-existing finalized external rubric.

## Working competency criteria

| ID | Provisional competency | Observable performance / evidence | Episode 01 contribution | Episode 02 contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | Constraint definition | Names the goal, one bounded rule, a justified exclusion, and an exception path | Why constraints enable disciplined performance | Model roles and right-sized tool access |
| C-02 | Human authority | Names who may propose, who may verify and who can approve; records a human selection rationale | Creative spark, editorial/script lock | A=ChatGPT, B=Human, C=Gemini; human resolves disagreement |
| C-03 | Evidence literacy | Distinguishes a model claim, source provenance, empirical observation and unsupported inference | Verification throughout disciplined practice | Model agreement does not prove truth |
| C-04 | Corrective feedback | Identifies a failed condition, narrows cause and specifies a changed test or constraint | Wrong-topic image/overly rigid render cases | Challenge/reconcile two proposals |
| C-05 | Traceability | Produces a prompt → response → human decision → plan → observed evidence chain with explicit HOLD states | Repeatable governed workflow | Two-model review and decision ledger |
| C-06 | Comparative judgment | Defines what would falsify claimed gains in accuracy, cost, time or quality, naming confounders | What counts as results? | Single-model vs human-directed triad evaluation |
| C-07 | Transferability | Gives another learner sufficient versioned context to inspect or continue a decision | Discipline sustained over time and archival practice | Handoff that a successor can audit |

All IDs are **local proposed IDs**, not inherited canonical identifiers.

## Suggested performance levels — candidate

| Level | Label | Scoring guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | NOT DEMONSTRATED | Missing, fabricated or materially incorrect explanation and no meaningful evidence |
| 1 | DEVELOPING | Recognizes a concept but cannot apply it independently or distinguish evidence from assertion |
| 2 | APPLIED WITH SUPPORT | Applies to a grounded scenario with prompts/scaffolding; documents decisions and unresolved checks |
| 3 | INDEPENDENT & DEFENSIBLE | Applies independently to a real/specified case, supports claims with source/test evidence, identifies failure and correction |

**Possible threshold (not adopted):** Level 2 or better for C-01 through C-07 with source/evidence integrity non-negotiable. A threshold only becomes binding after explicit human editorial approval and suitable assessment validation.

## Candidate assessment packet — produce alongside the audio

- **Listening reflection:** Explain the difference between discipline and repeating an error; cite the source-grounded case.
- **Constraint card:** State learner project objective, bounded tools/permissions, why one alternative was excluded, and exception path.
- **Triad decision record:** For a serious project, capture two model proposals (real or clearly labeled synthetic), human interpretation, specific decision, verification plan and disposition.
- **Evidence ledger:** Link prompt version, actual response or supplied quotation, rationale, source/test evidence and status.
- **Transfer exercise:** Another learner reads that ledger and lists what can be reproduced and what remains UNKNOWN.

A learner may demonstrate the same competency through multiple routes; assessment must test the **same actual criterion**, not assume equal mastery from equal listening time.

## Production evidence checklist per episode

For each draft chapter record: `episode_id`, `chapter_id`, `competency_ids`, `teaching_claims`, `source_refs`, `learner_action`, `assessment_item`, `missing_operand`, `review_decision`. Review for omissions and contradictions. An episode segment can build several competencies, but should have a primary target.

**Change control:** Maintain rubric versions with old/new criteria and why they changed. Distinguish a learner-friendly phrasing improvement from an actual reduction or change in the assessment standard.

## Evidence boundaries

The repository currently contains Day Zero instructional cases involving human correction, role permissions, source congruence, and deterministic QA. These motivate candidate outcomes, but **do not constitute a ratified complete rubric or measured educational effectiveness**. This rubric is co-developed in response to explicit human direction; it is not derived from a previously authenticated official assessment.

**Milestone:** Human reviews the destination statement and working criteria while drafting Episode 01. When approved for production, freeze a version for that script, continue developmental work toward Episode 02, then test whether the assembled audio curriculum supports the same competencies. Formal learner-outcome validation follows actual assessment data.
