## Course purpose

The educational goal is *constraint and discipline through human-led feedback, evidence and time*. The technical archives are concrete experiences by which those concepts can be taught. Do not treat prompts, model responses or a podcast itself as proof of learning.

These two episodes complement rather than duplicate one another:
- Episode 01 asks: **How do deliberate constraints, practice, feedback and verification produce accountable outcomes?**
- Episode 02 asks: **What does a human actually do when coordinating two models with partly overlapping capabilities and errors?**
