# BOSS — Day Zero Audio Overview Source Brief
Version: 0.1 candidate · 2026-10-10
Status: SOURCE PACKAGE — not a produced audio recording
Audience: beginners learning governed AI-tool workflows before Day One Windmill practice.

## Primary teaching objective
Explain how a human operator coordinates AI systems without confusing model output, agentic harness control, execution environment, and verifiable outcomes.

## Narrative sequence
1. **Opening — From Passenger to Pilot in Command.** Introduce the difference between receiving AI-generated content and governing a multi-tool workflow.
2. **Picard and Data as an instructional analogy.** Jean-Luc Picard represents accountable human command authority; Data, a fictional android, provides an analogy for specialized technical capability and analysis. Do not imply real AI is sentient or has personal intention. Do not recreate Star Trek dialogue or copyrighted scenes.
3. **Harness definition.** "An execution and control framework that connects a system under operation to its tools, environment, instructions, permissions, state, and verification mechanisms, enabling repeatable, observable, and bounded behavior."
4. **Four-part relationship.** The model generates outputs; the harness manages execution controls; the environment runs permitted operations; the human authorizes purpose and boundaries. Explain that tools have observable statuses rather than emotions or beliefs.
5. **Commercial examples.** Codex and Claude Code combine models with surrounding agent execution loops, tools, permissions and feedback. BOSS proposes a portable governance harness; that commercial positioning is a proposal, not validated market superiority.
6. **Day Zero practical exercise.** Manual copy-and-paste from one AI interface into another. Demonstrate a structured instruction with source, output contract, evidence request, and UNKNOWN/NOT ESTABLISHED handling. Contrast a claim of execution with a linked and independently inspected execution record.
7. **Research example — Windmill.** Assignment 05 operator-reported five policy classifications correct; Assignment 06 seven-fixture enforcement package committed. Orchestration halt, authenticated approval pause and production authorization remain NOT ESTABLISHED until observed. Do not misrepresent these as established.
8. **Transition to Day One.** Introduce Windmill as an optional orchestration environment whose free access enables learner practice; Day Zero should remain focused on local-device preparation and manual handoffs.

## Structural-Correspondence Firewall
People exercise judgment, authority, responsibility and consent. Software processes inputs, generates outputs and has states, permissions, execution traces and observable behavior. Use Split-Signal Error (verified output/evidence contradiction), Evidence Deficit (missing support), Authorization Failure, and Control Violation; do not attribute human cognition or psychological symptoms to software.

## Canonical architecture references
BOSS Constitution = limits and responsibilities. IPOS = input/process/output/storage correspondence. PARCELS layers: PLATFORM, ATTACHMENT, ROUTING, CARRIAGE, EXCHANGE, LANGUAGE, SERVICE, with GOVERNANCE spanning all layers. PRIME = Package → Route → Inspect → Move → Establish Delivery.

## Audio style and scope
Educational two-host discussion: one host asks practical beginner questions, the other explains with precise technical language and vivid analogies. Natural spoken English, not a promotional pitch. Suggested 12–18 minutes; use source priority over time target. Introduce jargon with plain explanations. End with three reflective prompts: who authorizes, what is verified, what remains UNKNOWN?

## Boundaries
No invented test results, no fabricated Windmill run history, no biological personification of AI, no claims of legal certification or human-subject research findings. Star Trek comparison is an abstract educational analogy only.
