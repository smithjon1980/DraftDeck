## Blind spots to test rather than ratify
1. BOSS has extensive delivery/governance contracts but an incompletely tested buyer segmentation/offer design loop.
2. Perceived customer value is not the same metric as audited business cash yield: the book's heuristic and VALUE-001 MUST remain distinct.
3. Client burden and implementation delay should be measured explicitly in offer testing; not inferred from enthusiastic testimonials.
4. Guarantees create operational and legal obligations; no promise before ability-to-fulfill checks.
5. Scarcity/urgency can become deceptive; verify real limits and avoid artificial pressure.
6. The book does not establish BOSS capability safety, source authenticity, acceptance testing, or licensing rights. These remain independent BOSS controls.
