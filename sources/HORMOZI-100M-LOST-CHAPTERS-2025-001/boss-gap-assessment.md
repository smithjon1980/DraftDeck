# Cycle 03 — Lost Chapters: structural correspondence
Status: research-only / NOT_TESTED

This volume explicitly identifies four topical areas: promotion types, customer-financed acquisition economics, advanced offer sequences, and employee management. BOSS may learn from their *questions* without accepting sales claims or importing marketing templates.

| New candidate concern | BOSS correspondence | Failure to probe |
|---|---|---|
| Customer-fit segmentation | SEVEN Survey / commercial discovery | demographic/protected-trait targeting or unverified 'best customer' assumption |
| Premium/free/discount decision | honest-offer-presentation and suitability | misrepresented promotion or unsustainable acquisition |
| CAC, LTGP, cash payback | VALUE-001 and financial custody | conflating gross revenue with margin, estimates with settled cash |

| Subsequent offer sequencing | COM-016 outcome-qualified expansion | upsell without a separate customer need |

| Maker-manager tasks | SOHO-specific human authority | implicit enterprise staffing or unaccountable delegation |

## Three high-priority uncovered blind spots
1. **Cash timing versus profitability:** eventual LTGP can be positive while near-term cash cannot sustain acquisition. Track both payback time and minimum liquidity.
2. **Customer segmentation ethics and validity:** observations about high-paying clients do not justify using sensitive identity categories or discarding customers without a fair business-relevance test.
3. **Commercial continuity obligations:** recurring or lifetime promises create future delivery liabilities that need explicit cost and exit conditions.

## Not adopted
No universal endorsement of premium offers, artificial urgency, automatic upsells, lifetime pricing, franchise-scale staffing or claim that customer-funded acquisition is viable for every neighborhood shop. Original quote/diagram material excluded from this public candidate.
