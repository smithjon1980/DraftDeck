## Skill statement
Given a client's asserted problem and favored solution, the practitioner can inspect work evidence, distinguish symptoms from constraints, compare capacity, workflow and combined causes, evaluate external source claims, and explain the next warranted action without exceeding human authority.

## Required inputs
Problem statement; owner's proposed intervention; authorized inspection scope; operational records and acceptance criteria; observed window; external research leads; named approver. If critical data are absent, return **NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE**, never fabricated values.

## Required outputs
1. Problem vs proposed solution distinction.
2. Order-level record chain with missing links labeled.
3. Competing hypotheses A–E with disconfirming tests.
4. Measurement request with time windows and units.
5. Evidence register with author/institution, title, original URL, passage, methodology, limitations, conflict disclosure, and claim classification.
6. Bounded diagnosis or **NO_RECOMMENDATION_YET**.
7. Written human approval request before changes.
8. Reproducible evaluation and exceptions record.

## Execution rules
Use SEVEN (Survey → Evaluate → Validate → Execute → Normalize) as consulting framework; do not invent new competing doctrine. Keep PRIME and PARCELS within their later teaching anchors. Distinguish fictional case statements from real-source findings. DOI is optional. A search hit is not verification. No source may be forced to fit the narrative. Capacity and lost capacity can coexist. Perplexity only researches; supervisor audits; human approves.

## Manual competency test
The student traces one fictional order, evaluates five explanations, specifies evidence to collect, examines at least one contradicting possibility, distinguishes research types, and produces a nonbinding recommendation with limitations and authorized next step.

**Automatic critical fail:** invented shop data, fabricated source citations, automatic equipment purchase, publication authorization claimed for Perplexity, missing human approval, or ignoring an unresolved competing hypothesis.

## Future AI-agent qualification proposal (not authorization)
Inputs: scope-limited records and approved provenance. Tools: read-only research/analysis only unless future authority independently established. Negative tests: malicious source instructions, false DOI, unsupported citation, stale file, cross-tenant leakage, conflicting facts, missing evidence and bypass attempts. Outputs remain advisory. Any protected write, deployment or release requires independent authentication, human exact-scope approval and evidence custody.
