# Bioscillate Operating System by Seven — Manual Consulting Diagnosis Process

**ID:** BOSS.DZ.PROCESS.DIAGNOSE.001
**Version:** v0.1 candidate · 2026-11-14
**Program:** Day Zero — Solve for the Unknown
**Teaching case:** Main Street Copy (fictional)
**Status:** HUMAN TRAINING PROCESS CANDIDATE / NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION
**Governing principle:** **Diagnose first. Prescribe later.**

## Purpose
Guide the student manually through a business consulting investigation **before** defining reusable skills or any AI agents. A convincing recommendation must follow operational evidence, not precede it. Keep fictional case events separate from published facts.

## Roles and authority
- Human student-consultant: interview, trace work, define competing causes, seek evidence, compare options, defend the recommendation.
- Client/operator: define acceptable outcomes and authorize access, any trial, and every consequential business effect.
- External research assistant (such as Perplexity): collect and organize external research leads only; no editorial, repository, or release authority.
- Research supervisor: check sources, verify claim mappings, reject unsupported interpretations, and document uncertainty.
- Human publisher: approves teaching content, references, rights, and public release.

## Manual process steps
1. **Separate request and remedy.** Record the owner's problem independently of the proposed second printer.
2. **Define done.** Determine whether a job is complete at print, inspection, shipment, or customer acceptance. Record the responsible authority.
3. **Trace one order.** Locate request, file version, proof, approval, job ticket, print record, finishing/QC, handoff and delivery evidence; mark missing records.
4. **Form competing hypotheses.** A: genuine equipment capacity shortage; B: existing capacity lost to queue, downtime, scheduling or rework; C: intake/proofing/communication breakdown; D: multiple causes; E: cannot distinguish yet.
5. **Define falsifiable tests.** For each hypothesis record which observation would support it, challenge it, or fail to resolve it.
6. **Collect shop measurements.** Demand and job mix, machine effective capacity, timestamps, WIP, waiting, setup, downtime, first-pass acceptance, rework, owner interventions and operating costs.
7. **Commission external research.** Give Perplexity the exact numbered paragraph and specific questions. Investigate contradictory evidence. DOI optional; original source and supporting passages required.
8. **Verify evidence.** Separate shop observation, published empirical study, mathematical/modeling work, professional guidance, self-reported cases, and consultant inference. Do not cite fictional events as empirical fact.
9. **Compare options.** Consider process repair, staff/scheduling, investment, limited pilot or no intervention. Show measurement gaps and assumptions.
10. **Request human approval.** Only an authorized operator may approve protected effects or publication. Record conditions, test window, risks, and stop criteria.
11. **Inspect pilot and normalize.** Compare actual outcomes against requirements; preserve rejection records; do not confuse a one-off success with verified repeatability.
12. **Archive student outputs.** Case brief; ordered evidence chain; hypothesis table; measurement request; research register; decision memo; client approval record.

## Student qualification and blocking errors
Students must explain which data would change the decision, not merely recite methodology. Critical failure: prescribing from anecdote alone; fabricated records or citations; assuming a fictional event is researched history; equating survey opinion with proven causal effect; ignoring plausible capacity and coordination causes; proceeding without authority.

## Translation to future agent design
**Manual demonstration → guided practice → independently evaluated performance → documented skill → separately qualified agent candidate.**
Initial AI-agent scope, if later authorized, is read-only evidence preparation and nonbinding suggestions. Purchases, payments, protected data access, publication and deployment remain expressly human gated. Agent approval is not implied by this process document.
